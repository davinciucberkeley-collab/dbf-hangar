- **Edited test `test_gate_skip_without_allowable` (now al-6061-t6):** acceptable. - **Flexural vs tensile modulus for tip deflection:** flexural is the right choice. The spar is loaded in bending and flexural modulus is measured in bending. At 127.6 GPa it is below the tensile 134.4 GPa, so the deflection is conservative (about 5% larger). The same holds for strength: flexural 1379 MPa is below tensile 1655 MPa and covers the compression side, which usually governs a pultruded tube in bending. The `rule` text still says "Young's modulus"; the source text says flexural, which is enough.
