## Gaps and friction (what I tried, what failed, what the docs did not say)
1. **No servo with `frames` fits a 1:10 wing.** Only `emax-es08maii-metal` (12 g, 24 mm tall) and `emax-es3054hv` have frames. Aileron bay: 0.802 of the servo body inside the wing, so it pokes about 4 mm out of the upper skin. The micro servos in the library (feetech, pololu, powerhd, es9051) have no frames, so `design place` cannot use them. `knowledge/parts/README.md` says frames are optional but not that this blocks every micro servo.
2. **`design place` is only for a servo normal to a skin.** On the hollow tail it put the elevator servo on the fuselage top (body-inside fraction 0.17, "inside host" means inside the shell volume) and the rudder servo on the belly at z -42, far from the rudder. I set position and rotation by hand for the rudder servo (beside the fin on the stab, shaft toward the fin, arm side minus, horn toward 0,-1,0). The docs do not say what `--face lower|upper` means with a sideways `--shaft`, or how to mount on a hollow wall.
3. **An elevator with a gap around the fin is one part, so the hinge pivot must lie ON the elevator.** T1.articulation said "27.0 mm from elevator" for a pivot at y 0 (the gap, 24 mm + half the fin). Fix: pivot at y 100. Not documented that `elevator_fin_gap` leaves the centre empty, and for an articulated joint T1.attachment_location used the pivot, not `location` (changing `location` alone did nothing; about 3 wasted calls).
4. **Elevator hinge line is straight only when sweep matches taper** (stab_sweep_le_deg 5.9 for hinge_frac 0.6 with chords 120/92). With the real 10 deg LE sweep and 0 deg TE the hinge is a V and one revolute axis cannot follow it. The guide says tail surfaces do not follow `hinge_from` yet but not this consequence; for the rudder (tilted hinge) I computed the axis [0.2688, 0, 1] by hand. `hinge_from: generator` for tail surfaces is the biggest missing helper.
5. **Rudder base vs elevator.** The fin root is in the stab plane, so the swinging rudder cuts through the elevator centre. Only fix: a 24 mm gap in the elevator (unlike the real aircraft). Raising `fin_z_root` would disconnect the fixed fin. A rudder cut-out or fin base height param would help.
6. **add-linkage differential throws need trial and error.** Each of 4 surfaces took 3 to 5 runs of about 45 s. The guide says the sign "depends on arm side and servo orientation" but gives no rule. `follower_deg` is `null` whenever there is a jam, so you cannot see how far off you are; the jam text names the input only. Suggest: report follower angles on a jam and offer `--throw-up/--throw-down`. Default `--servo-range 30` made the T1 sweep collide the rod with fuselage and fin; 21 to 25 worked. The docs list the option but not this.
7. **`--deflection` plus offset undershoots** (documented) and `deflection_deg` is a mean, so "up 20 / down 15" cannot be requested. I ended at aileron 19.9/13.7 and elevator 28.3/20.4.
8. **Auto horn height gives 3 to 4 mm horns** when the servo is close to the surface (elevator 3.7 mm, arm 4.8 mm): jam-prone and unrealistic. `--horn-height` fixes it, but the doc only says "2.5 to 40 mm", with no sensible default for a 1:10 model.
9. **Sign of `--station`.** Negative station means +y for hinge axis [0,-1,0] (not stated; "mm along the hinge axis from its pivot"). My first elevator linkage ran the rod through the fin and the rudder arm side "+" put the rod into the stab; both fixed by trial (offset servo to y 10-20, station -85, arm side minus).
10. **Library mesh does not match its bounding box at the contact check.** `design bounds` for `rx` (radiomaster-rp3) says z -55.1..-51.1, y +-6.5, but T1.attachment_location reported the joint 3.5 to 4.5 mm from the receiver for every point on the centre line and cleared only near the y edge. The battery top is about -55 in T1 against -47.9 in bounds. I found no CLI read-out of the true surface; probing cost about 10 calls.
11. **Hollow fuselage makes joints hard.** A point in the cavity is "N mm from fuselage". Every internal part (battery, ESC, receiver, servos) needs its joint point on the shell wall, and `embed_ok` has a 35 % overlap limit. The guide documents `wall` but not this consequence. A tray or bulkhead part, or a "mounted in cavity" mode, is missing. The battery rests on the belly curve at its aft end only.
12. **Landing-gear leg top is not where the parameters suggest.** Formula track/2 - (attach_z - axle_z) tan(splay) gives y 54.7 at splay 35; T1 measured about 43 (10 mm from the fuselage). I dropped splay to 30 and moved the joint. The generator doc does not say where the leg meets the airframe, and one joint point serves both main legs.
13. **Schema version trap.** `design new --blank` writes 1.2. `add-linkage` (four_bar), `continuous` joints, `wheels: separate` and `display` need 1.3; the failure is two T0.version FAIL rows inside the add-linkage output with no hint to run `design set schema_version`.
14. **`design apply` verbs.** `set` refuses a missing key, `add` refuses an existing one; I hit both in five of the apply files. A single put verb would have saved about 5 failed applies.
15. **Relative path.** `design apply --edits e.yaml` resolves against the repo root, not the cwd. Clear error, but undocumented.
16. **Timing.** add-linkage and validate take 45 to 55 s each; two linkage runs in one shell call passed the 120 s default and were backgrounded. The docs do not give typical times.
17. **My own slip:** I ran `python -` once while inspecting the schema; it read stdin and hung (the harness did not let me kill it). A stray python.exe may remain. No data involved.
18. **Fuselage tail vs elevator.** The elevator clipped the tail cone (838 mm3) until I narrowed the tail stations and added the gap. Wings have `cut_files`; tail surfaces have no cut around a fuselage.
19. **The SKIP count is a poor work-left number.** Of 80 SKIPs, about 70 are joint-rating placeholders, 5 are T1.kinematic_link (expected) and 5 are VTOL rows not applicable.
20. **`baseline update` is needed** (76 known rows) although validate shows 0 FAIL, because SKIPs make the design not accepted. The guide says so; `design propose` does not run it and does not warn.
21. **FEA:** `fea run --all` says OpenCAE not found on this machine; T2.fea_strength SKIP for the one printed part (gear).
22. **Mass realism.** 629 g at 1:10 with a 270 g battery and 97 g motor; the only 4S battery and the only motors in the library are heavy for a 1.1 m model. MTOW 1157 g is the scale-law figure, not a team number.

## What worked well
- `design bounds` is the fastest sanity check and belongs in the team guide.
- `design apply` with a generated YAML (60 sets in one pass) and the "moved N parts, interference new/cleared" line after each edit were the most useful feedback.
- `add-linkage` output (ratio, tip, pin, rod, T6 rows) and `sim mechanism` made throw tuning feasible.
- Wheel joints, hinge bevel and gap, and wiring waypoints were right first time; T7 accepted the routes after two re-routes.
- `hinge_from: generator` on the ailerons followed the wing (dihedral included) with approximate typed values.
