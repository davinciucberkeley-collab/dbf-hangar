### Blocking issues
1. **docs-research-aerodynamics.md:13 (Summary).** The entry says "The v0.3.1 geometry failed the stall and endurance requirements mainly because of bare round landing legs". The source blames the legs only for the drag and endurance failure: CD0 0.081 against 0.059, the legs adding 0.036, so 9.1 min against R10's 10 min. The stall miss has a different cause, a trimmed CL_max of 1.13 against the 1.2 sizing assumed, giving 12.4 m/s against 12. The source marks it "R11 at risk (3 % over)" (verdict table line 13), and the fix was re-sizing the wing on the trimmed CL_max (line 23). Make the legs the cause of the endurance failure only, and give CL_max as the cause of the stall miss.
2. **docs-research-configurations.md:17-21 (Key facts 2-6).** These facts quote the source's "Verdict (first run, v0.3.1 inputs)" and sections 2.4-2.5 without saying so: the 30 deg against 9 deg gust tilt, the infeasibility list, the 0.0237 of CD0 0.059 (40 %), 10.1 and 8.8 min, the 14.3 min and +$94.17 for folding props, and the joiner reserve factor 1.85 to 1.61 and 1.51. They sit next to v0.4 figures (line 16; the 7.35 and 6.30 on line 17). The source says that the sections after "Current results" "record the first run of this study, on the v0.3.1 inputs". In the v0.4 run, for example, one forced-pack variant (C0-pk-HOOVO6S8000, $1,964.04) is feasible, but line 18 says "big packs" exceed the cap. Label these facts as first-run (v0.3.1) values.
3. **docs-research-firmware.md:13 (Summary).** The entry says the source "records thirteen design decisions". The source has fourteen (D1 to D14, section 3). The same sentence says "Simulator runs passed all seven scripted scenarios" but drops the caveat the source gives in its section 5 Limits: the SITL airframe is ArduPilot's generic QuadPlane, and the runs "do not validate gains, power or endurance". Correct the count and add the caveat.
4. **docs-research-propulsion-margin-data.md:20 (Connectors).** The entry says "an XT90 is rated 45 A continuous and 90 A for a minute on 10 AWG, so 82 to 98 A is beyond it". The source (section 4) says the XT90 "is over its 45 A continuous rating but within its 90 A, 1-minute rating only up to 90 A". The entry makes it sound as if both ratings are exceeded across the whole range.
5. **docs-research-team-crosscheck.md:16 (X1).** The entry says "the bay was only 5 mm longer than the pack". The source (section 2.1) says the bay was cut 165 mm long for the 155 mm pack, so it is 10 mm longer and the pack can slide +/-5 mm (about +/-0.55 mm of CG), against the build manual's +/-30 mm slide.
6. **docs-research-tolerance-analysis.md:16.** The entry says "Carbon and aluminium tube tolerance ... a nominal 25.4 mm tube spans 25.197 to 25.603 mm". The source works only with drawn 6061-T6 tolerances (section 2 table, "Tubes (drawn 6061-T6)") and says in its opening paragraph (line 7) that "the tubes in the BOM are drawn 6061-T6 aluminium, not carbon". Drop "Carbon and".
7. **docs-research-tolerance-analysis.md:19.** The entry says "insert bores should be 4.0 to 4.2 mm, 7.5 mm deep". In the source's section 7 list, "X to Y" means a change from X to Y (compare "M5 nut pocket 8.0 to 8.4" and "Splice plug 23.2 to 23.0"). The fit table has F-14 "4.0 -> 4.2", and the verdict says "the 4.0 insert bores are wrong". The corrected bore is 4.2 mm, not a 4.0-4.2 range.
