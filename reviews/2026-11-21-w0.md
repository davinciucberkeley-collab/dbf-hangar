### Required fixes
1. `core/hangar/ledger.py:166` (`decided = decided_in or item.decided_in`). A drop can be recorded with a decision reference that was given for a different status change. Criterion: brief "`dropped` and `superseded` need `decided_in`" (an argument of `set`), and plan Review Focus #5 ("`dropped` to require `decided_in:`", i.e. the decision that dropped it). 2. `core/hangar/cli.py` `ledger_report`: overdue formatting and `prompts.jsonl` parsing/counting are business logic in a click handler. Fix: move both into `ledger.py` (e.g. `report(self, today: str | None = None, prompts: Path | None = None) -> str`; the no-argument call stays valid), leaving the CLI as `click.echo(led.report(today, root / "ledger/prompts.jsonl"))`.

2. Decision items could take `owner` from the §3 "ask" cell (e.g. `team`). The brief is silent; harmless as is.
3. Re-seeding never updates the status of a decision whose plan row changed to "decided" (e.g. D10 at 12-01). Record decisions with `plane ledger set D10 decided --evidence …`, or let seed promote `open` → `decided` only (never downgrade).
4. `_commit` duplicates `_save`'s own "assign only on success" behaviour; harmless.
5. `ledger.py` imports PyYAML internals (`yaml.nodes`, `yaml.resolver`) for `_quoted`; fine, but they are worth a comment if the PyYAML pin ever moves.
