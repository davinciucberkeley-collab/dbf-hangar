## Summary
Lift fans buried in the wing and covered in cruise. The study drops it: hover power at high disk loading is high, the covers need actuators, and no autopilot mode supports covered fans; it is kept in the list for completeness. The morph cell is not approximate.

## Key facts
- Lift fans are buried in the wing and covered in cruise [MORPH.md §3.1 row K17]
- High disk loading makes hover power high, and the covers need actuators [MORPH.md §3.1 row K17]
- Cost is over $2,000 (ESTIMATE), because hover power pushes the design to 6S and larger packs [SCREENING.md row K17]
- Build time is 10 or more weeks [SCREENING.md row K17]
- The MORPH row cites an engineering thesis from Embry-Riddle as its origin [MORPH.md §3.1 row K17]

## Status in the study
DROP. Reason: high disk-loading hover uses up the pack and the budget; no autopilot support for covered fans. No card exists, so no judging scores.
