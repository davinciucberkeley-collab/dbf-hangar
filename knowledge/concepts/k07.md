## Summary
A quad with two front motors on tilting nacelles at the wing leading edge and two fixed rear lift motors on short booms that stop in cruise. The screen treats it as K06 with one extra rear station, and kept it only as a fallback if K06's yaw and pitch authority tests short. The wing cell follows the study's W1 code, and the tail is not mapped, so the entry carries no tail cell. The morph cells are exact.

## Key facts
- Cost $1,597 in the screening cost model, about $48 more than K06 [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K07].
- Same servos as K06 and the same fatal rear motor-out in hover [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K07; concepts/MORPH.md §3.1 row K07].
- The layout is a Nimbus-class design from a source snippet that was not verified [concepts/MORPH.md §3.1 row K07].
- Binary tilt servos that lock at both ends remove the loaded-servo hold failure, at the cost of intermediate tilt angles [concepts/AUDIT-LOG.md, evidence table row "K06 / K07 / U05 tilt servos"].

## Status in the study
MERGE into K06: the same mode as K06 with one more station; choose it only if K06's yaw and pitch authority tests short [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K07]. No card; judging scores none.
