## Summary
Two stacked wings joined by vertical struts, with a motor at each strut end, so the box stands on its lower wingtips; it is a stiff foam-board build. The study merges it into K09 as a planform option, because the box may remove the field joint entirely. The biplane's lift-to-drag loss is an estimate. The morph cell is not approximate.

## Key facts
- Two wings joined by vertical struts; a motor at each strut end; stands on its lower wingtips [MORPH.md §3.1 row K11]
- Same tailsitter gust weakness as K09, with a shorter span [MORPH.md §3.1 row K11]
- Cost is about the same as K09, with one box and no joint, and a build time of 3–5 weeks [SCREENING.md row K11]
- A research prototype flew in a copter-style flight stack with no transition code; the audit log prefers the ArduPilot copter-tailsitter mode [AUDIT-LOG.md evidence table, row K11]

## Status in the study
MERGE→K09. Reason: a planform option on K09 that may remove the field joint; the study plans to build it as its foam test model. No card exists, so no judging scores.
