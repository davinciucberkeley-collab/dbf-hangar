## Summary
A survey of existing buyable VTOL airframes against the budget, with notes on transport, endurance and supply. The file calls itself a recovered summary: the full tables and sources were not saved. Its airframes are mainly tilt-rotors and quadplanes, which map to the tilt-rotor and lift-plus-cruise cells.

## Key facts
- The HEEWING T2 Cruza VTOL (1.2 m tri tilt-rotor) is listed at $795 with case and US stock, and is the only buyable airframe that leaves about $600 of the $2,000 for spares (ESTIMATE). [FINDINGS.md, point 1]
- The 2.1 m MakeFlyEasy Striver Mini is about $858 plus UK shipping and leaves under $200. [FINDINGS.md, point 1]
- A $150 FIMI Manta or a $360 T1 Ranger is named as a cheap ArduPilot trainer. [FINDINGS.md, point 2]
- Quadplanes are the best documented layout; their weak points are wing twist, boom flex and arm hinges, while tilt-rotors fail at tilt servos and transition tuning. [FINDINGS.md, point 3]
- Commercial transport cases are 1.0 to 1.4 m long, so a 2.0 to 2.2 m span in two wing halves fits a car. [FINDINGS.md, point 4]
- Catalogue endurance should be cut by 2 to 3 times, and Foxtech VTOLs are China-only, with new Chinese export licences on drone parts starting 18 Nov 2026. [FINDINGS.md, points 5 and 6]
