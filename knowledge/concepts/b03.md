## Summary
B03 buys an EPO 1.3 m flying wing and converts it into a quadplane by adding two carbon booms with four lift motors. The source calls it a well-trodden ArduPilot conversion with over 300 forum posts. The airframe kit is out of stock, so it is treated as an airframe-source option for the K01 layout rather than a separate design. The cells follow the source's code (lift-plus-cruise, fixed wing, V-tail).

## Key facts
- EPO 1.3 m airframe with two carbon booms and four lift motors; the forum thread has over 300 posts. [MORPH.md §3.2 row B03]
- Kit about $150, out of stock; availability tagged UNVERIFIED. [MORPH.md §3.2 row B03]
- Cost about K01 minus $100 for the airframe, plus the $150 kit, tagged ESTIMATE. [SCREENING.md row B03]
- 1.3 m wing, 2 boxes; 0 loaded mechanisms; 4–6 weeks. [SCREENING.md row B03]
