## Summary
U02 is a tailsitter whose quad sits inside a ring wing of about 0.5–0.6 m diameter, with the motors on four internal struts. It packs into one box with no joint, and the source cites a peer-reviewed paper on hover and forward flight, but its aerodynamic performance is poor. The source's ring-wing code (W6) and tail code (C4) have no hangar option, so the entry carries only the tailsitter cell; this is approximate for the lift dimension.

## Key facts
- Ring wing of about 0.5–0.6 m diameter, printed or foam, with motors on four internal struts; sits on the ring's trailing edge. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U02]
- Low aspect ratio gives poor lift-to-drag, and the largest side area in hover gusts of any tailsitter in the set. [SCREENING.md row U02]
- Peer-reviewed annular-wing VTOL paper (2020) documents hover and forward flight; the entry used the abstract only, as the full page could not be opened. [AUDIT-LOG.md evidence table, row U02]
- Ring-wing needs a printed ring over 300 mm for the 4–6 week estimate; one box, no joint. [SCREENING.md row U02]

## Status in the study
DROP: poor lift-to-drag and large gust side area for a transport gain that K11 gives more cheaply; K09 covers the cell. [SCREENING.md row U02]
