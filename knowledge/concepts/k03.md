## Summary
A variant of the K01 quadplane in which the lift motors sit on one carbon H-frame through the fuselage, with a V-tail on a single boom and a one-piece wing. The screen gives it about the same cost as K01 and merged it into K01, leaving the boom attachment to detail design. The morph cells are exact.

## Key facts
- Lift motors on a carbon H-frame that unbolts as one unit; the wiring for the lift motors never crosses the wing joint [concepts/MORPH.md §3.1 row K03].
- One-piece wing up to 1.5 m, two boxes for transport [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K03].
- Cost about the same as K01; build 6 to 8 weeks with no loaded mechanisms [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K03].
- Built as conversions of two existing airframes (Mini Talon and Skywalker X8 class), per the cited sources; those sources were not opened for this entry [concepts/MORPH.md §3.1 row K03].

## Status in the study
MERGE into K01: the boom attachment choice is made in detail design; the clean wiring path is a real plus [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K03]. No card; judging scores none.
