## Summary
A class-S quadplane with a 1.6 m wing, two carbon booms carrying four lift motors, a V-tail and a pusher on a central pod, packed in one box. It is the shortlist winner that the earlier team sized end to end, so it has the fewest unknowns. A skeptic review of its card lowers the hover and cost margins, but the layout itself is judged the best-documented VTOL path. The morph cells are exact.

## Key facts
- Wing span 1.60 m, area 0.40 m², 4S pack; packed as one 1.2 × 0.5 × 0.4 m box with about 7 kg total [cards/K01/CARD.md §Key numbers].
- MTOW of 3.22 kg, which includes the 0.25 kg growth allowance (ESTIMATE, mass build-up) [cards/K01/CARD.md, Key numbers table, MTOW row].
- Hover thrust-to-weight 1.96 on the motor table and 1.83 with battery sag; the card's own target is 2.0 [cards/K01/CARD.md §Key numbers].
- Card cost $1,741.68 with a $258 reserve; the screening run priced it at $1,636 with a $364 reserve [cards/K01/CARD.md §Key numbers; screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K01].
- The skeptic review corrects mass and hover margin (T/W 1.83, or 1.70 with sag), and puts the cost near $1,897 with about $103 reserve [cards/K01/REVIEW.md §5].
- Judges note the wing as drawn has no full-span spar and the centre of gravity and tail still need a fix [judging/build-and-transport.md, K01 row].
- A 2026 forum thread proposes a tilting pusher on a 4+1; no frame class supports it and it was not flown [concepts/AUDIT-LOG.md, evidence table row "K01 (pusher)"].

## Status in the study
KEEP (shortlist S1): passes every gate with a reserve, and it is the layout with the most sized detail [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K01]. Judging scores: build-and-transport 4.0 (rank 7 in that table); flight-risk 7.0 (rank 3); mission-and-learning 8.0 (rank 1) [judging files, K01 rows].

## Open questions
- Wing maximum lift coefficient 1.1 is an estimate [cards/K01/CARD.md §Key numbers].
- Pusher static thrust of about 1.4 kgf is unverified until measured on a thrust stand [cards/K01/CARD.md Top 3 risks].
- The planned flight-controller board has a Plane firmware target only in beta [cards/K01/CARD.md §Controls].
