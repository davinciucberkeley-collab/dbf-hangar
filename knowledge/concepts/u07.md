## Summary
K01 with a small high-C LiPo for the lift motors and a bought Li-ion pack for the pusher and avionics, meant to give a longer cruise without a new airframe. The source says the split pays only above about 15 minutes of cruise, so for the basic demonstrator it adds wiring and cost with no return. The screening merges it into K01 as an option. The morph cell is the K01 cell and is not approximate.

## Key facts
- The lift motors run on a small high-C LiPo, and the pusher and avionics run on a bought Li-ion pack. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U07]
- Adds about $70 to $140 for the Li-ion pack and a second battery monitor on top of K01. [SCREENING.md, row U07]
- Pays only when cruise is above about 15 minutes (ESTIMATE), which the source ties to the growth mission M-B. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U07]
- The source's pack reference is a trade-study Li-ion pack that was over the weight cap on C0. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U07, Source column]
- Its stated benefit is an energy-dense cruise pack as a growth path that needs no new airframe. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U07, Why column]
