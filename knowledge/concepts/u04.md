## Summary
U04 is K01 built from paper-faced foam board cut from laser templates, with wing panels that slide on carbon spars so a crashed panel can be re-cut in about an hour. It is a material and manufacturing option on K01, scored for repair speed and build hours. Its main doubts are foam stiffness and weight at 3–4 kg and water at the bay-shore site. The cells follow K01's code (lift-plus-cruise, fixed wing).

## Key facts
- K01 built from paper-faced foam board, laser-cut templates, wing panels ride on carbon spars, and a damaged panel takes roughly one hour to cut again. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U04]
- Cost about K01 minus $40 of foam, tagged ESTIMATE; 4–6 weeks; 1 h panel repair. [SCREENING.md row U04]
- Repair speed and build hours are the reasons it is kept; the source scores them with weights 15 (schedule) and 20 (buildability). [MORPH.md §3.3 row U04]
- Kill risks: foam stiffness and weight at 3–4 kg, and water sensitivity at the bay-shore site. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U04]
