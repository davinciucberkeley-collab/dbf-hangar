## Summary
B06 is a programme plan, not a configuration: buy a cheap VTOL aircraft (B01) to fly for pilot and parameter practice while a scratch main aircraft is built, reusing its radio and charger. The budget only fits a mule below about $350 and a main aircraft in the class-S scratch layouts. The cells come from the codes of the mule (B01) and of K01 only, as the brief's mapping rule requires; the main aircraft is not yet fixed, so they are not a layout choice.

## Key facts
- B01 is acquired in December and flown in January during the main aircraft's build, giving pilot practice, ArduPilot VTOL parameter experience and failsafe tests. [MORPH.md §3.2 row B06]
- The mule costs $410–$550 with packs, and the main aircraft must then fit about $1,300–$1,400, which leaves only class-S scratch layouts and a thin reserve. [MORPH.md §3.2 row B06, Screen column]
- SCREENING gives the mule as $300–550 and the total as $1,800–2,050, a different mule figure from MORPH. [SCREENING.md row B06]
- The main aircraft can be K09, K06 or a K19-class quadplane. [MORPH.md §3.2 row B06]
- Leaves under $200 for a crash in the SCREENING arithmetic. [SCREENING.md row B06]
