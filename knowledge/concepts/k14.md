## Summary
A front and a rear wing joined by tip plates, with lift motors at the four joints. The study drops it: half-chord panels lose lift-to-drag at low Reynolds number, there is no build evidence, and it fixes nothing this mission needs. The morph cell is not approximate.

## Key facts
- Front and rear wings joined by tip plates; lift motors sit at the four joints [MORPH.md §3.1 row K14]
- The layout is based on a box-wing design paper by Schiktanz and Scholz [MORPH.md §3.1 row K14]
- Cost is about K01 plus tip plates, or $2,111 at class M [SCREENING.md row K14]
- Half-chord panels lose lift-to-drag at low Reynolds number [SCREENING.md row K14]
- Build time is 8–10 weeks, with extra tip-plate joints [SCREENING.md row K14]

## Status in the study
DROP. Reason: no dedicated autopilot mode and no documented build; nothing it fixes for this mission. No card exists, so no judging scores.
