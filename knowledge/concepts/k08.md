## Summary
A layout in which all four rotors tilt, so none is parked in cruise. The screen dropped it because it needs four loaded tilt mechanisms and has no clean documented transition, and it gives nothing K06 lacks. The morph cells are exact.

## Key facts
- All four rotors tilt for cruise; there is no parked rotor [concepts/MORPH.md §3.1 row K08].
- Four loaded servos; one forum builder hit roll and yaw inversion and stopped [concepts/MORPH.md §3.1 row K08].
- Cost about $1,650, estimated as K07 plus two tilt servos [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K08].
- Infeasible at the 6 kg class in the earlier trade study [concepts/MORPH.md §3.1 row K08].
- Build time 8 or more weeks [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K08].

## Status in the study
DROP: two soft fails (four loaded mechanisms and no clean documented transition) with no gain over K06 [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K08]. No card; judging scores none.
