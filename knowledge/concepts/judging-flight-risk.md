## Summary
This lens asks how likely a first-time team is to fly each aircraft successfully and safely, covering hover, transition, cruise, landing, wind, autopilot maturity and failure modes. It scores seven concepts from 0 to 10. The ranking, first to last, is U01 at 8.0, B08 at 7.5, K01 at 7.0, K19 at 6.0, B09 at 5.0, K06 at 4.0 and K09 at 3.5, and the scores lean toward crash cost and time on the airframe.

## Key facts
- Ranking from first to last: U01 8.0, B08 7.5, K01 7.0, K19 6.0, B09 5.0, K06 4.0, K09 3.5. [flight-risk.md §1 ranking table]
- Every concept is fatal on a hover motor-out except K01 with its hexa option. [flight-risk.md §4 item 1]
- Corrected hover T/W is K09 2.6, U01 2.2, B09 2.15 at its worst motor, B08 about 2.0, K01 1.70 with sag, K19 1.6 at low voltage, and K06 1.53 to 1.62. [flight-risk.md §4 item 2]
- In an 8 m/s gust, K09 leans 36 degrees on the broadside wing, the worst in the set. [flight-risk.md §2 wind row]
- Every controls-maturity cell rests on forum evidence rather than a documented flight log. [flight-risk.md §4 item 8]
