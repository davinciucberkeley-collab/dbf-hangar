## Summary
A tailless quadplane with four lift motors on a centre section and two elevon-driven outer panels, sized as a scratch foam build on the X8 family. The screen keeps it beside K01 because the wing form changes packing, stall and crash behaviour rather than duplicating K01. The morph cells follow directly from the code string, so none is approximate.

## Key facts
- Span 1.60 m, wing area 0.496 m², MTOW 3.10 kg with a 3.26 kg mass gate [cards/K19/CARD.md, Key numbers].
- Total cost 1,655.60 USD incl. tax and shipping, reserve 344.40 USD; the screen had priced it at 1,592 USD [cards/K19/CARD.md, Key numbers; AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K19 row].
- Hover thrust-to-weight 2.04; cruise 165 W at 16 m/s; cruise endurance 17.4 min with 20 % reserve, hover-only 8.5 min [cards/K19/CARD.md, Key numbers].
- Packs into one box of 1.2 × 0.5 × 0.4 m; five field joints, one person, about 5–8 min to assemble [cards/K19/CARD.md, Transport and assembly].
- A bought X8 shell was rejected: builder X8 quadplanes weigh about 5.1 kg, which gives T/W 1.24 on class-S stations [cards/K19/CARD.md, Key numbers].
- Build about 95 h hands-on plus about 40 h of unattended printing [cards/K19/CARD.md, How it is built].
