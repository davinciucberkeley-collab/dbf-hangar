## Summary
A tricopter with two front motors that tilt on servos for cruise and one fixed rear motor, on a flying wing of about 1.3 m span, built from scratch. It is the cheapest tilt layout in the screen and stands for the tilt family. Its thrust-to-weight margin is thin, and the skeptic review places it at the 1.6 gate by end of pack. The wing cells follow the study's W1 and W2 code; the tail cell follows its C3 code.

## Key facts
- Take-off mass about 2.39 kg; span 1.30 m, area 0.338 m² [cards/K06/CARD.md §Key numbers].
- Hover thrust-to-weight 1.98 on the motor table and 1.79 with sag; the review gives 1.62 fresh and about 1.53 at end of pack [cards/K06/CARD.md §Key numbers; cards/K06/REVIEW.md §Numbers I believe (corrected)].
- Card cost $1,649 with $351 reserve; the review gives about $1,705 and $295 reserve. The screen priced it at $1,549 [cards/K06/CARD.md §Key numbers; cards/K06/REVIEW.md §Numbers I believe (corrected); screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K06].
- The screen row says K06 is $113 under K01, but its own figures ($1,549 against $1,636) differ by $87 [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K06].
- Endurance on the card is 2 min hover, then 17 min cruise; the review corrects cruise to about 9.4 min after 2 min hover [cards/K06/CARD.md §Key numbers; cards/K06/REVIEW.md, row C10].
- Packs as one box of 0.88 × 0.50 × 0.39 m; option K07 adds a rear motor for +$78 [cards/K06/CARD.md §Key numbers].
- A tilt-servo binary mode in ArduPilot removes the loaded-servo hold failure at the cost of intermediate tilt angles [concepts/AUDIT-LOG.md, evidence table row "K06 / K07 / U05 tilt servos"].
- Tilt nacelles are team-designed, needing about three print iterations; build about 180 h plus 3 to 4 weeks of tilt tuning [judging/build-and-transport.md, K06 row].

## Status in the study
KEEP (shortlist S3): the cheapest tilt layout and the representative of its family; the card must stay in its weight class to keep thrust-to-weight [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K06].
