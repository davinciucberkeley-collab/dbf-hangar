## Summary
Buy the design files for a printed VTOL and print the airframe on campus printers, rather than design the airframe. The card sizes the Flightory Stallion tilt-tricopter; the Eclipson tailsitter is named but not sized, since no file price was found. The morph cells cover both codes: the Stallion gives tiltrotor, fixed-wing and v-tail, and the Eclipson gives tailsitter, flying-wing and no tail.

## Key facts
- Total cost 1,546.29 USD incl. tax and shipping, reserve 453.71 USD; the screen had estimated 1,250–1,400 USD [cards/B09/CARD.md, Key numbers table; SCREENING.md row B09].
- MTOW about 2.30 kg, span 1.34 m, one packed box of 1.09 × 0.44 × 0.31 m [cards/B09/CARD.md, Key numbers table].
- Printing takes about 100 printer-hours, with lightweight PLA for the skins and PETG for the hard parts [cards/B09/CARD.md, Key numbers table and How it is built].
- Files are licensed for personal use only, so the source advises asking the designer before a university capstone uses them [cards/B09/CARD.md, Top 3 risks].
- The Stallion ships with ArduPilot parameter files and a flight controller output map; the Eclipson build log reports yaw problems and crashes during tuning [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions B09 row; cards/B09/CARD.md, Controls].
- Judging: build and transport 5.0 (rank 5), flight risk 5.0 (rank 5), mission and learning 4.5 (rank 5) [judging/build-and-transport.md §3; judging/flight-risk.md §1; judging/mission-and-learning.md §2].

## Status in the study
KEEP (SCREENING.md row B09): a different buy path with a flown aircraft and supplied parameters; the kill risks, LW-PLA stiffness and heat plus the licence, can be checked in week 1. The card's build-and-transport judging is conditional on Jacobs Hall's policy on bring-your-own filament.
