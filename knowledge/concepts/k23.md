## Summary
A folding-wing VTOL in which the wings fold about skewed hinges so that four wing-mounted motors form a quad in hover, and all four keep pulling in cruise. The morph cell is approximate: the source's lift code L8 has no row in the hangar chart, so vtol_method:lift-plus-cruise is the closest cell, although the folding wing is not a lift-plus-cruise layout. The lift:fixed-wing cell comes from the W1 code.

## Key facts
- Total cost 1,610.52 USD, reserve 389.48 USD; four lift stations, one fold actuator priced as a tilt servo, four control servos and three packs [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K23 row].
- The folded state is also the transport state, so the box needs no field joint [SCREENING.md row K23; AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K23 row].
- A printed hobby reference of about 1.6 m span uses one servo that drives the fold through a cord and pulley [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K23 row].
- Hard fail on a maintained autopilot mode: no native ArduPilot mode, so the fold has to be faked as a binary tilt or scripted [SCREENING.md row K23; AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K23 row].
- Two student projects, in 2024 and 2025, failed to find hinge angles that bring the wings fully vertical [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K23 row].
- A company patent is noted; the source says to check it before a university project [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K23 row].

## Status in the study
DROP (SCREENING.md row K23): hard fail on a maintained autopilot mode, plus hinge geometry risk. The source keeps a foam fold mock-up (under 40 USD) as a side project only. Not carded, so no judging scores.
