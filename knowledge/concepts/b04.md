## Summary
B04 turns a cheap foam FPV flying wing into a twin-motor tailsitter by mounting two motors on the leading edge and using fins as landing legs. It is the cheapest airframe in the set, and it inherits the same motor-out and wind weaknesses as K10. The audit log records a published build of this type that flew and transitioned. The cells follow the source's code (tailsitter, flying wing, no tail).

## Key facts
- Cheap EPP flying wing (class of ZOHD or AtomRC, tagged UNVERIFIED) with two motors on the leading edge and fins as legs. [MORPH.md §3.2 row B04]
- Reference thread: 1 m span, 10 in props, 1.38 kg build reported. [MORPH.md §3.2 row B04]
- Airframe under $150, tagged ESTIMATE; total about $1,100, tagged ESTIMATE; wind risk the same as K10. [SCREENING.md row B04]
- A published 845 mm build with two motors on printed tilt mounts and two micro servos weighed 650 g, flew and transitioned in 2020; its printed pivot blocks broke in early mishaps. [AUDIT-LOG.md evidence table, row B04]
- The same build page says moderate to heavy wind is a persistent problem for tailsitters, and that the thrust-vectored belly form is the one that flies. [AUDIT-LOG.md evidence table, row B04]

## Status in the study
MERGE->K10 (dropped): the cheapest airframe in the set, but it carries K10's fatal motor-out and wind problems. If the team wants a trainer under $150 this is the option; it is not the demonstrator. [SCREENING.md row B04]
