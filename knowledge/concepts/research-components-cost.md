## Summary
A cost roll-up of the study's layouts into price bands, with the reserve left after one bad crash, plus the rule-of-thumb costs per motor and per avionics item. The file calls itself a recovered summary written after the research agent could not save its full report. Its layouts span quadplanes, tailsitters and tilt-tricopters and tilt-quads.

## Key facts
- Layouts at 2 to 2.5 kg, 4S, span at most 1.5 m total $1,492 to $1,636, with a reserve of $364 to $508. [FINDINGS.md, totals table, first row]
- A 4 to 6 kg 6S quadplane 4+1 totals $2,003 (reserve −$3); a 6S octo 8+1 totals $2,350 (reserve −$350). [FINDINGS.md, totals table, rows 2 and 3]
- One bad crash costs about $309, and the fixed floor is about $600 ($213 avionics, $265 ground kit, $124 wiring). [FINDINGS.md, paragraph after the totals table]
- Totals include a 10.25 % Berkeley tax rate, shipping, spares and a ground kit. [FINDINGS.md, opening paragraph]
- Each lift motor costs about $40 at 2 to 2.5 kg or $68 at 4 to 6 kg. [FINDINGS.md, implications list]
- ELRS MAVLink telemetry costs $0, airspeed is optional, and the design should stay at or under 12 flight-controller outputs. [FINDINGS.md, implications list]
