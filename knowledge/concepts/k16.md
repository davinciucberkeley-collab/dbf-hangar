## Summary
A V-22-style layout with two tilting rotors, where hover pitch comes only from the tilt. The study drops it: any motor-out is fatal, no documented hobby transition exists, and it carries the highest control risk in the set. Its class S cost is an estimate. The morph cell is not approximate.

## Key facts
- Two tilting rotors; hover pitch is produced by tilt only [MORPH.md §3.1 row K16]
- Cost is $2,156 at class M [SCREENING.md row K16]
- Needs two tilt servos and a build of 8 or more weeks [SCREENING.md row K16]
- The ArduPilot autopilot lists the layout in type 3 and class 10, and a 2022 builder abandoned the ArduPilot setup [SCREENING.md row K16]

## Status in the study
DROP. Reason: any motor-out is fatal, there is no documented hobby transition, and it is the highest control risk in the set. No card exists, so no judging scores.
