## Summary
A quad tailsitter wing that carries four motors, with the fuselage hung on a free pitch pivot so the body stays level while the wing settles into the wind; a small tail motor trims the body in hover. No other row lets the wing and body move relative to each other passively, and the only hobby-scale evidence is hover-only research hardware with no ArduPilot or PX4 mode. The screening merges it into U01 as the free-spar variant of the one-day foam test. Morph cell is approximate: the source code is an L6/L3 hybrid, L6 (wing-assisted quad) has no exact chart cell, and the free-pivot wing option (W8) is not in the chart.

## Key facts
- Four motors sit on the wing, and the fuselage hangs on a free pitch pivot at the wing's aerodynamic centre; a tail motor trims the body in hover. [AUDIT-LOG.md, additions table, row U09]
- The cost model totals $1,591.85 with a reserve of $408.15 (four lift stations on the wing, one cruise and tail station, two elevon servos, three packs); the pivot bearing and encoder are not in the model. [AUDIT-LOG.md, additions table, row U09]
- The hardware evidence is the Colibri research aircraft (ICUAS 2024), 1.7 kg, with a 12-bit encoder on the pivot and elevons; it made a 2 m climb and a 54 s hold, hover only, and flew no transition. [AUDIT-LOG.md, additions table, row U09]
- The source documents wing-body coupling oscillations and weak disturbance rejection in hover. [AUDIT-LOG.md, additions table, row U09]
- The screened cost is U01 plus about $30 for the pivot. [SCREENING.md, row U09]

## Status in the study
MERGE->U01 (test variant): a hard fail on autopilot support as a flight article; it survives only as the free-spar variant of U01's one-day foam test. [SCREENING.md, row U09]
