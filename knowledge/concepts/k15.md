## Summary
A quadplane with eight lift rotors, for tolerance to a hover motor-out. The study drops it: the cost is over the raw $2,000 figure, and the motor-out benefit is available more cheaply as an option on K01. The morph cell is not approximate.

## Key facts
- Eight lift rotors give hover motor-out tolerance [MORPH.md §3.1 row K15]
- Cost is $2,350 by the study's cost model [SCREENING.md row K15]
- Build time is 8 or more weeks, and the layout is supported in ArduPilot class 3 and 4 [SCREENING.md row K15]
- The MORPH row cites a previous trade study's C1 and C2 layouts as its origin [MORPH.md §3.1 row K15]

## Status in the study
