## Summary
A tricopter-style quadplane with three fixed lift motors, the rear one on a tail boom that also serves as the yaw actuator, plus a pusher. It fills the cheaper-than-4+1 region of the set. The morph cells follow from the code string (L1, W1, C2), so none is approximate.

## Key facts
- Total cost 1,609.48 USD, reserve 390.52 USD; three lift stations, one cruise motor and five servos including the yaw servo [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K22 row].
- One lift station fewer than K01; the audit log puts the saving near 36 USD of goods, the screen puts it near 27 USD against K01 [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K22 row; SCREENING.md row K22].
- The quadplane frame guide lists a tricopter frame class for this layout [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K22 row].
- The layout has no hover motor-out tolerance, and the yaw servo carries load on the tail motor [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K22 row].

## Status in the study
DROP (SCREENING.md row K22): saves little against K01 and adds a loaded yaw servo and an unflown layout; two soft fails. Not carded, so no judging scores.
