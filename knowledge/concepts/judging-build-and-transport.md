## Summary
This lens asks whether a student team can build, transport, repair and maintain each shortlisted aircraft inside $2,000 and by the end of April 2027. It scores seven concepts on five sub-scores that sum to 10. The ranking, first to last, is B08 at 9.5, U01 at 9.0, K09 at 8.5, K19 at 5.5, B09 and K06 tied at 5.0, and K01 last at 4.0.

## Key facts
- No scratch card prices its transport box, which the reviews put at about $20 to $60 each. [build-and-transport.md §5 item 1]
- Carbon tubes are the common long-lead part: K01, K19, K06 and K09 buy them from overseas, and K01 and K19 buy no spare. [build-and-transport.md §5 item 2]
- The flight controller is backordered on five of the seven cards. [build-and-transport.md §5 item 3]
- Maker Passes are in no bill of materials, and the cost is unverified for spring 2027. [build-and-transport.md §5 item 4]
- The field's flight-request lead time of 7 business days means a one-day repair still costs about 1.5 weeks of calendar. [build-and-transport.md §1 and §5 item 5]
- Mass headroom matters for repair: K06 at a T/W of 1.53 to 1.62 has none to spare for the tape and glue a repair adds. [build-and-transport.md §5 item 7]
