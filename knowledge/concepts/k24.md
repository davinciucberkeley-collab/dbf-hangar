## Summary
A reverse tricopter in which one front motor tilts 90 degrees for cruise and two rear motors, sized for hover, stop in cruise and vector for yaw. It is the mirror of K06 with a different motor split. The morph cells come from the code string, where the C code gives tail:none and the C1 part has no row, so it is skipped.

## Key facts
- Total cost 1,579.59 USD, reserve 420.41 USD; three lift stations, three loaded servos priced as tilt servos, two control servos and three packs [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K24 row].
- The ArduPilot frame table has a dedicated frame type for this layout, added by a 2019 pull request [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K24 row].
- A 2019 forum builder recorded hovers with wings in August 2019, then two forward transitions that failed in late August and early September 2019, with the nose rising during transition [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K24 row].
- The screen notes three loaded mechanisms and a 7–9 week build [SCREENING.md row K24].

## Status in the study
DROP (SCREENING.md row K24): three loaded mechanisms and the weakest transition record of any tilt row; K06 covers the three-motor tilt cell with better evidence. Not carded, so no judging scores.
