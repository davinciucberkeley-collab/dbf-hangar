## Summary
U01 is a bought X500-class quadcopter with a foam wing fixed at about 25 degrees to the rotor plane, flown in ArduCopter with no transition mode. The card puts the forward-flight current saving near half; the skeptic review in the same folder re-runs the model and puts it nearer one fifth, which is the decisive number for the day-one test. The review also finds that, as specified, it does not meet the transition mission. The cells are approximate: the source's L6 code has no hangar cell, so it maps to none and multirotor; the wing maps to fixed wing.

## Key facts
- Base: 500 mm carbon X frame, four lift stations, a 1.2 m foam wing in two halves; card take-off mass 2.71 kg. [CARD.md key figures table]
- Hover about 356 W and 24 A, thrust-to-weight about 2.2, tagged ESTIMATE. [CARD.md key figures table]
- Card: at 14 m/s the quad draws 9.4 A against 18.0 A bare (−48 %). The review's corrected run gives about 24–25 % in the model, and cites a flight-test abstract for about 19 % at a similar setting. [CARD.md key figures table; REVIEW.md §0 and §1]
- Cost: the card gives $1,523 including tax and shipping, leaving $477 for crashes; review's corrected committed cost about $1,626, leaving about $374. [CARD.md key figures table; REVIEW.md §6]
- Build 55–65 h and 2–3 weeks in the card; review revises to about 70–85 h and 3 weeks. Packed as one box, card 0.65 × 0.44 × 0.38 m, review 0.80 × 0.44 × 0.38 m. [CARD.md Build and key figures; REVIEW.md §5 and §7]
- Controls: ArduCopter QUAD/X with no transition logic; review rates control risk 2 of 5, and notes the wing's lift changes sign at low speed. [CARD.md Controls; REVIEW.md §4]

## Status in the study
KEEP (conditional on a one-day test): the lowest control risk of any wing-carrying option, cheapest scratch path; the wing's payoff is settled by a foam test before ordering. Judging scores from the three judging files: flight-risk 8.0 (rank 1 of its list), build-and-transport 9.0 (rank 2), mission-and-learning 2.5 (rank 7).
