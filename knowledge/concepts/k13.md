## Summary
Two short wings, with the lift motors on the four wingtips and no booms, and a pusher at the tail; the short span packs easily. The study drops it: there is no documented transition, pitch stability is sensitive to centre of gravity, and the packing gain is small compared with K01's halves. The morph cell is not approximate.

## Key facts
- Two short wings; lift motors on the four wingtips, no booms; pusher at the tail [MORPH.md §3.1 row K13]
- Short span packs well into the transport box [SCREENING.md row K13]
- Cost is about the same as K01 at class S, and $2,015 at class M [SCREENING.md row K13]
- Build time is 7–9 weeks [SCREENING.md row K13]
- Pitch stability depends strongly on centre of gravity, and the autopilot has no dedicated mode for it; a similar layout was moved to a copter setup [MORPH.md §3.1 row K13; SCREENING.md row K13]

## Status in the study
DROP. Reason: no documented transition and centre-of-gravity-sensitive stability on a first-time team's only airframe; the packing gain is small against K01's halves. No card exists, so no judging scores.
