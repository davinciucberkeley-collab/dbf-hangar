## Summary
This lens asks what each aircraft can demonstrate on the VTOL demonstrator mission and how far it can grow, using four weighted sub-questions. It scores seven concepts from 0 to 10. The ranking, first to last, is K01 at 8.0, K09 at 7.0, K06 at 6.5, K19 at 6.0, B09 at 4.5, B08 at 3.0 and U01 at 2.5, with U01 ranked last because it never transitions.

## Key facts
- The demonstrator mission is a 2 minute hover, a cruise of 5 minutes at threshold and 10 minutes as the objective, a 20 percent reserve and a 0.25 kg growth allowance. [mission-and-learning.md, opening section on what the lens scores]
- K01 corrected cruise is 15 to 20 minutes after a 2 minute hover, with a hover-only endurance of about 7 minutes. [mission-and-learning.md, K01 reasons, sub-score A]
- K09 corrected cruise is about 19 minutes (12 minutes pessimistic), with hover-only endurance of 13 minutes. [mission-and-learning.md, K09 reasons, sub-score A]
- K06 corrected cruise is 9.4 minutes, just under the objective, with hover-only endurance of 6.3 minutes. [mission-and-learning.md, K06 reasons, sub-score A]
- U01 scores 1 on the transition sub-question because its rotors carry at least 75 percent of the weight at every speed. [mission-and-learning.md, U01 reasons, sub-score A]
