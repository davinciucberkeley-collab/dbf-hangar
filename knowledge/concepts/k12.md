## Summary
The whole wing pivots between hover and cruise, carrying two to four motors, with a small tail fan for pitch in hover. The study drops it: the wing pivot carries full lift load, autopilot support is thin, and only one documented build exists. Its cost at class S is an estimate. The morph cell is not approximate.

## Key facts
- The whole wing with two to four motors pivots; a small tail fan gives pitch in hover [MORPH.md §3.1 row K12]
- The MORPH row cites tilt-wing reference designs (a GL-10 type and a CL-84 type) as the basis for the layout [MORPH.md §3.1 row K12]
- Cost is over $2,000 at class M (old study: $2,149) [SCREENING.md row K12]
- The main risk is one pivot carrying the full lift load; only a flap-type tilt-wing mode is supported by the autopilot [SCREENING.md row K12]
- Build time is 10 or more weeks [SCREENING.md row K12]

## Status in the study
DROP. Reason: a loaded wing pivot, thin autopilot support and one build; the old study found it infeasible. No card exists, so no judging scores.
