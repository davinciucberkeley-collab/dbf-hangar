## Summary
A flying wing with two large props, where the elevons in the propwash do all hover control. The study drops it: any motor-out is fatal in both modes, and it costs more than the single-motor tailsitter K09 for the same wind problem. The evidence table in the audit log records that the vectored belly-sitter form is the one that flies and lands better in wind; that form is kept as a landing option, not as a new row. The morph cell is not approximate.

## Key facts
- Layout is a wing with two large motors; hover control comes from elevons in the propwash, and it stands on its tail in hover [MORPH.md §3.1 row K10]
- Cost is $1,601 in the study's cost model, which is $109 more than K09 [SCREENING.md row K10]
- Weathervane crashes are documented for this layout, and any motor-out is fatal [MORPH.md §3.1 row K10]
- The ArduPilot tailsitter guide defines a vectored belly-sitter form for twin motors that lands flat in wind; the audit log treats it as landing option G6 [AUDIT-LOG.md evidence table, row K10 / B04]
- The build length is 5–7 weeks and no team-made servos are needed, but two tilt servos are needed for the vectored form [SCREENING.md row K10]

## Status in the study
DROP. Reason: a motor-out is fatal in both modes and it costs more than K09 while facing the same hover-in-wind issue, and K09 already covers the tailsitter cell. No card exists, so no judging scores.
