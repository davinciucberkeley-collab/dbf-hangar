## Summary
A bought, ready-to-fly tilt-tricopter with no structure built by the team, sized as the cheapest and fastest-to-fly option. The screen named the FIMI Manta or ZOHD Altus, but the B08 card makes a HEEWING T1-S the primary aircraft, with the Altus as the alternative and the Manta as a backup. The morph cells come from the code string (L2, W1, C2), so none is approximate.

## Key facts
- Screen price about 950–1,100 USD total [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions B08 row]; the card prices the T1-S at 1,388.75 USD incl. tax and shipping, reserve 611 USD, because it uses the scratch floor [cards/B08/CARD.md, Key numbers and top note].
- Built-up T1-S take-off weight about 0.69 kg, hover T/W 2.4 on the card's own figures, cruise about 24 min after 2 min hover [cards/B08/CARD.md, Key numbers table].
- One person, one tote, about 3 min to assemble with no tools [cards/B08/CARD.md, Transport and assembly].
- About 80–100 team-hours, with first hover in build week 1–2 [cards/B08/CARD.md, How it is built].
- The board is an official ArduPilot board, and a community parameter file exists; the card records that this resolves the open question on the bundled flight controller [cards/B08/CARD.md, top note].
- The card names capstone content as the main weakness: the team designs almost nothing structural [cards/B08/CARD.md, Top 3 risks].
