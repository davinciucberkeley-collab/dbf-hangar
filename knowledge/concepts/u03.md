## Summary
U03 is K09 with every moving surface removed, so all control comes from differential thrust in both modes. It has the fewest parts and is fast to repair, but its roll and yaw authority in cruise is small. The source notes that a published quad tailsitter of this kind flies when ArduCopter-style stabilisation is kept in fixed-wing modes. Since this is the same concept as K09 with a different servo count, the cells match K09's tailsitter and flying-wing cells.

## Key facts
- K09 with every moving surface removed; all control by differential thrust in hover and cruise. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U03]
- Zero servos, so the fewest parts and the fastest repair; foam-board build in hours. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U03]
- Roll and yaw authority in cruise from differential thrust is small at high speed; the source puts the thrust-to-weight need at 2:1. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U03]
- A published H-Wing thread flies a quad X motor tailsitter with no control surfaces, keeping copter stabilisation in fixed-wing modes. [AUDIT-LOG.md evidence table, row U03]
