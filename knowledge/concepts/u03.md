## Summary
U03 is K09 stripped of all moving surfaces, so control comes entirely from differential thrust in both modes. It has the fewest parts and is fast to repair, but its roll and yaw authority in cruise is small. The source notes that a published quad tailsitter of this kind flies when ArduCopter-style stabilisation is kept in fixed-wing modes. Since this is the same concept as K09 with a different servo count, the cells match K09's tailsitter and flying-wing cells.

## Key facts
- Take K09 and remove every moving surface: all control then comes from differential thrust in hover and cruise. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U03]
- Zero servos, so the fewest parts and the fastest repair; foam-board build in hours. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U03]
- At high speed, roll and yaw control from differential thrust in cruise is weak; the source puts the thrust-to-weight need at 2:1. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U03]
- A published H-Wing thread flies a quad X motor tailsitter with no control surfaces, keeping copter stabilisation in fixed-wing modes. [AUDIT-LOG.md evidence table, row U03]
