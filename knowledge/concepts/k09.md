## Summary
A flat flying wing with four motors in an X, two above and two below the wing on fins, that stands on its fins and pitches over for cruise. It has no control surfaces and is the cheapest, fastest scratch build in the set, so the screen kept it. Its known weak points are wind tilt on the bay-shore site and fatal motor loss, which the card treats as a test-site rule and a risk. The morph cells are exact.

## Key facts
- Wing 1.15 m by 0.30 m, one piece in one box with no structural field joint [cards/K09/CARD.md, opening description].
- Take-off mass 2.38 kg; the skeptic review corrects it to 2.43 kg [cards/K09/CARD.md §Key numbers; cards/K09/REVIEW.md, section 2 challenge table].
- Hover thrust-to-weight 2.65 on the table and 2.47 with sag; cruise lift-to-drag 6.0, or 5.0 pessimistic [cards/K09/CARD.md §Key numbers].
- Hover lean is 16° at 5 m/s and 37° at 8 m/s wind, past the default 30° limit [cards/K09/CARD.md §Key numbers].
- Card cost $1,528.51 with about $471 reserve; the review gives about $1,632 and $368. The screen priced it at $1,492 [cards/K09/CARD.md §Key numbers; cards/K09/REVIEW.md §1; screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K09].
- Build of about 70 hands-on hours, about 3 weeks to first hover (ESTIMATE) [cards/K09/CARD.md §How it is built].
- A motor failure is fatal in both hover and cruise [cards/K09/CARD.md, Top 3 risks].

## Status in the study
KEEP (shortlist S4): cheapest and fastest scratch build with no servos; its wind weakness is handled by a morning-flight rule and a 5 m/s wind limit [screening/SCREENING.md §3 row K09].
