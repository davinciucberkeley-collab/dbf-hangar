## Summary
A profile flying wing stood on its tail with one motor and no thrust vectoring, which is the only single-rotor option in the set. It is the cheapest propulsion in the study but has no hover yaw authority and no logged transition was found. The morph cells come straight from the code string, so none is approximate.

## Key facts
- Total cost 1,568.80 USD, reserve 431.20 USD; one lift motor, one ESC, four control servos and three 6S packs [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K20 row].
- ArduPilot's tailsitter guide documents the type with its own frame and tailsitter parameters, and it states that a single-motor tailsitter is a valid 3D-capable type [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K20 row].
- The screen gives $1,569 for this layout and records no hover yaw authority without vectoring [SCREENING.md row K20].
- A 2019 forum developer advised adding two wing motors with pitch vectoring, which turns the design into K09 [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K20 row].

## Status in the study
DROP (SCREENING.md row K20): cheapest propulsion, but zero hover yaw authority and no documented transition; the developer's advice points to K09, which covers the tailsitter cell. Not carded, so no judging scores.

## Open questions
- No logged hover-to-cruise transition of a single-motor tailsitter on ArduPilot was found in the research round; this is marked UNVERIFIED [AUDIT-LOG.md, Additions K20 row].
