## Summary
K01 with sacrificial printed feet on the boom ends and two small wheels, so that a failed hover can end in a rolling landing and a crash repair takes minutes. The source offers it as the landing-gear answer to the mentor's wheels-versus-skids question. The screening merges it into K01 and defers the choice until the field grass has been walked. The morph cell is the K01 cell and is not approximate.

## Key facts
- The boom ends carry sacrificial printed feet, and two small wheels allow a rolling fixed-wing landing after a failed hover. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U08]
- The claimed benefit is crash repair in minutes and an added emergency landing mode. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U08, Why column]
- The wheels add mass and drag, and the field grass may be too rough to roll on. [MORPH.md §3.3 row U08, What kills it column]
