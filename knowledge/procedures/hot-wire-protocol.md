## Purpose
Hot-wire cutting of foam cores: electrical set-up and the stop rule for practice cores. Run before cutting and for every practice core.

## Steps
1. Set up the hot-wire electrical circuit as the set-up table specifies [G2#4]
2. Use the lowest current that cuts cleanly [G2#4]

## Checks and stop conditions
- Practice stop rule: repeat until two consecutive cores pass steps 2-4, or three cores have been used [G2#11]

## Notes
- (K09-L value) 24 AWG Ni80 at about 1.62 ohm/ft; 1.25 m span R = 6.6 ohm; start 2.5 A, V = 16.6 V, P = 41 W [G2#4]
- The set-up table is from a secondary source read on 2026-11-13, not a datasheet [G2#4]
