## Purpose
Safe charging of flight packs, and what to do after a crash, smoke or fire. Run at every charge and on any battery incident.

## Steps
1. Name one battery lead and a second person before any charge, re-termination or field session; nobody charges alone [H3#71]
2. Set the charger to balance mode for the LiHV chemistry [H3#21]
3. Check the charger's cell count and per-cell target on screen before starting [H3#21]
4. Start the charge [H3#24]

## Checks and stop conditions
- If the pack is dented, puffed, hot or smelling: unplug it, put it in an ammo can or in sand outdoors, observe it for at least 30 min, and never charge it again [H3#64]
- Smoke or fire: do not carry the pack; smother it with sand; call 911 if it spreads [H3#69]

## Notes
- (K09-L value) Target 4 cells at 4.35 V per cell, shown before start; charge current 1C = 6 A [H3#21]
- (K09-L value) About 59 min per pack from the 20 % line (EST, unverified) [H3#24]
