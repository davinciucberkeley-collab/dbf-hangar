## Purpose
Measuring the wind at the flying site over the flying window. Run on the mornings before the test window.

## Steps
1. Hold the handheld anemometer at 2 m [H3#4]
2. Take a 60 s average every 10 min over the window [H3#4]
3. Repeat the measurement on at least three mornings [H3#4]
