## Purpose
The RC range check at set positions, and the short daily check. Run for the full range test and before each flying day.

## Steps
1. Walk the range check at four headings, each at two attitudes, with the props off at 20 % [F5#15]
2. Run the daily short range check at 100 m, with one heading per attitude [F5#18]

## Checks and stop conditions
- Daily short check passes only when link quality is at least 0.9 and there is no failsafe [F5#18]

## Notes
- (K09-L value) 25 mW and 150 Hz for the range positions; 25 mW for the daily check [F5#15] [F5#18]
