## Purpose
Laying the pod down after landing, and inspecting it after a hard landing or tip-over. Run after every landing.

## Steps
1. Lay the pod flat, pod up, with the props spanwise first [C5#76]

## Checks and stop conditions
- After a hard landing or tip-over, inspect the shoe bonds, fin tips, wingtip caps, props, mounts, pod nose and block [C5#78]

## Notes
- (K09-L value) Lifts at 11.3 m/s, as stated [C5#76]
- The inspection list refers to section 6 of the source document, which gives no number [C5#78]
