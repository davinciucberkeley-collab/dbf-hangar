## Purpose
The motor time-constant test, which gives the motor thermal resistance and time constant. Run on the motor under hover power.

## Steps
1. Run the time-constant test at hover power for 600 s [E3#31]
2. Log the thermocouple every 5 s during the run [E3#31]
3. Fit the motor thermal resistance R_th and time constant tau from the log [E3#31]
