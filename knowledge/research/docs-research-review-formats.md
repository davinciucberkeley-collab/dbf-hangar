## Summary
A 2026-10-26 comparison of 16 published design-review formats (NASA review guidance, AIAA and SAE competition reports, university capstones, open hardware docs) against the DBF-Combined review page. It lists what the page already has, where it goes beyond the benchmarks and fourteen gaps. No morphological cell applies because the file is about review documents, not configuration.

## Key facts
- Strengths the source credits: a revision and sign-off block, a numbered requirements table with status, a risk register, a regulatory checklist, trade studies, a drawing set and mass and CG roll-ups, and a flight-test matrix with entry, abort and success criteria. [review-formats.md section 2 first list]
- Strengths beyond the benchmarks: live numbers regenerated by continuous integration, interactive CAD and CFD views, a mesh-uncertainty study, and source lines under every table. [review-formats.md section 2 second list]
- Largest gaps against the NASA entrance and success criteria: no criteria checklist, no verification-method or parent column in the requirements table, no technical-performance and margin summary, and a six-row risk register with no owners or matrix. [review-formats.md gaps 1-4]
- Further gaps: no action or finding log with closure evidence, no hazard analysis, no predicted-versus-measured slots, no consolidated references list, no management summary or roles, no one-page executive summary, no interface definition, no lessons learned, partial nomenclature and no pipeline view of simulation provenance. [review-formats.md gaps 5-14]
- Sources cited by DBF-Combined, not opened by this import: NASA NPR 7123.1D, NASA Systems Engineering Handbook sections, NASA S3001 risk guidance, AIAA DBF top design reports, Cornell and University of Washington DBF reports, SDSU and WPI capstones, SAE Aero oral score sheet, ConvertibleUAV docs, SimScale and Purdue course material. Some were read only as search snippets and marked unverified by the source. [review-formats.md section 1]
