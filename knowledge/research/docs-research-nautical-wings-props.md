## Summary
A 2026-10-19 look at a company named as a possible source of custom carbon propellers, a spec sheet to send any prop designer and acceptance tests for received props. The best match is an India-based electric motor maker; nothing found links it to free student prop design. No morphological cell applies because the file is about a supplier and prop procurement.

## Key facts
- The most likely match is Nautical Wings Aerospace of Bengaluru, founded in 2020, a business-to-business electric propulsion maker with a pre-Series A round reported in September 2024; it makes mostly motors and lists standard propellers from 14 to 40 in. [nautical-wings-props.md sections 1-2]
- No company website, layup details, hub or bore data, thrust data, lead time or prices were found for its props. [nautical-wings-props.md section 2]
- Spec sheet for a designer: lift props 17 in, 2 blades assumed, 2 clockwise and 2 counter-clockwise plus a spare each, adapter-mounted, hover thrust per rotor 1.545 kg (6.18 kg over 4); pusher 14 in with an 8.5 in pitch baseline; balance grade G 6.3 or better. [nautical-wings-props.md section 3]
- Lift props stop in cruise and must hold fore-aft; ESCs cannot park them, so the source prefers fixed blades and a mechanical parking method. [nautical-wings-props.md section 3]
- Acceptance tests listed: visual and tap check, mass check, static and dynamic balance, adapter runout, thrust-stand comparison against the baseline prop within the 26 A for 30 s motor limit, RPM step test behind a shield, tip clearance. [nautical-wings-props.md section 4]
- Sources cited by DBF-Combined, not opened by this import: idrw.org, Entrackr, Tyto Robotics customer story, ReadymadeRC product pages, Unmanned Systems Technology on balancing, ArduPilot Copter vibration pages, GetFPV. [nautical-wings-props.md sections 1-4]
