## Summary
Official German Armed Forces page on the A400M used by the German Air Force. It carries a technical data table with dimensions, masses, speeds, engine data and crew. The page is dated 16.04.2024 in its own text. Wing area and propeller data are not given.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Length 45.10 m; height 14.70 m; wingspan 42.36 m (technical data table).
- Empty weight 78.6 t; maximum takeoff weight 141 t; maximum payload 37 t (table).
- Engines: 4 x EPI TP400-D6, 8,250 kW (10,838 PS) per engine (table).
- Maximum speed 750 km/h at 10 km; cruise Mach 0.68 (table).
- Crew 3; cargo compartment capacity 340 m3 (table).
- Not stated on the page: wing area, propeller diameter, stall speed.

## How we could use it
- Second source for span, length, height, MTOW, empty mass and payload in the A400M reference sheet (knowledge/reference-aircraft/a400m.md). Span and length agree with the Wikipedia values to within 0.04 m.
- Gives the 8,250 kW per-engine power that conflicts with the 8,200 kW table value; both are recorded.
