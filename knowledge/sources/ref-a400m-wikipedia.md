## Summary
Encyclopedia article on the A400M Atlas: variants, first flight, and a General characteristics table that cites Airbus Defence and Space data. The article lists the production variant as A400M-180 Atlas. Category labels give the high-wing, four-turboprop and T-tail classes. The second half of the page (fetched at offset 100000) held references and categories only.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Length 45.1 m; wingspan 42.4 m; height 14.7 m; wing area 225.1 m2 (Specifications, General characteristics).
- Empty weight 78,600 kg; max takeoff weight 141,000 kg; max landing weight 123,000 kg; capacity 37,000 kg (Specifications).
- Engines: 4 x Europrop TP400-D6, 8,200 kW each (Specifications); design text gives 8,250 kW.
- Propellers: 8-bladed Ratier-Figeac variable pitch, 5.3 m diameter (Specifications).
- Cruise 781 km/h at 9,450 m (Specifications). First flight 11 December 2009 (Development/History text).
- Category labels: High-wing aircraft; Four-engined turboprop aircraft; T-tail aircraft (page categories, not a text description).
- Not stated on the page: wing sweep, dihedral, chords, tail dimensions, wheel track, wheelbase, stall speed, crew, CG range.
