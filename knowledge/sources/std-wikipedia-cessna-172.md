## Summary
Encyclopedia article with a general-characteristics and performance block. The specification block that was read is for the 172R, not the 172S, so its values are used only as second sources for dimensions and the propeller, and are labelled 172R. Wikipedia is a secondary source and is not authoritative.

## Usable rules / numbers
- 172R: length 27 ft 2 in (8.28 m); wingspan 36 ft 1 in (11.00 m); height 8 ft 11 in (2.72 m); wing area 174 sq ft (16.2 sq m). (172R specifications section)
- 172R: empty weight 1,691 lb (767 kg); gross weight 2,450 lb (1,111 kg); fuel 56 US gal (52 usable). (172R specifications section)
- 172R: engine Lycoming IO-360-L2A, 160 hp; propeller 2-bladed fixed-pitch metal McCauley 1C235/LFA7570, 6 ft 3 in (1.91 m) maximum diameter. (172R specifications section)
- 172R: never-exceed 163 kn IAS; cruise 122 kn; stall 47 kn (power off, flaps down); range 696 nmi; service ceiling 13,500 ft; climb 721 fpm. (172R specifications section)

## How we could use it
- Second source for span and length (two-source rule), and the propeller diameter and blade count for the `propeller` generator (planned U12.3), with the 172R caveat.
- Do not use the 172R mass or speed values for the 172S sheet.
