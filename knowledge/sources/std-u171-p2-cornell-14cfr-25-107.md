## Summary
Text of the Part 25 takeoff-speed rule as shown by the Cornell LII mirror. It gives minimum takeoff safety speeds as multiples of the reference stall speed V_SR. It contains no transition speed and no cruise-to-stall ratio.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [transition_stall_ratio_min] V2 minimum (takeoff safety speed) is "1.13 VSR" for two- and three-engine turbopropeller, reciprocating and turbojet airplanes without a significant one-engine-inoperative stall-speed reduction. Minimum (para. (b)(1)).
- [transition_stall_ratio_min] V2 minimum is "1.08 VSR" for turbopropeller and reciprocating airplanes with more than three engines, and for turbojets with such provisions. Minimum (para. (b)(2)).
- [cruise_to_stall_ratio_min] No cruise-to-stall ratio in the text read. V_FTO minimum is "1.18 VSR" (para. (g)(1)); takeoff, not cruise.
- The text uses V_SR, not V_S1. No 1.3 multiple appears in the text read.

## How we could use it
- Context only: these minimums are takeoff speeds, so they do not bound a transition-to-stall ratio or a cruise-to-stall ratio.
