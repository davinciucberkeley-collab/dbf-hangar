## Summary
Manufacturer product page for the A400M, with capability sections (heavy loads, outsize loads, aerial delivery, air-to-air refuelling, firefighting) and an FAQ. It gives payload, cargo hold size and volume, range with a 20 t load, speeds, engine family and propeller arrangement. It gives no length, span, height, wing area or MTOW. The page footer reads 2026; no version date for the specifications.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Maximum payload 37 tonnes (Heavy loads section).
- Cargo hold cross-section 4 m x 4 m; cargo hold volume 340 m3 (Outsize loads section).
- Range with 20 t load: 3,400 nm (Over longer distances section).
- Cruise Mach 0.68 to 0.72 (FAQ); maximum speed Mach 0.72 and altitude 40,000 ft (Strategic projection section).
- Four turboprops, named as Europrop TP400 in the FAQ.
- Counter-rotating propellers on each wing, inboard propellers turning inward and upward (FAQ).
- Minimum runway length less than 750 m (FAQ).
- Not stated on the page: length, span, height, wing area, MTOW, propeller diameter, crew, stall speed.

- Supports the payload, engine count and propeller arrangement fields of the A400M reference sheet (knowledge/reference-aircraft/a400m.md).
