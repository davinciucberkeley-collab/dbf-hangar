## Summary
Magazine column on hinge installation and control surface gaps. It says the gap between the mating halves should be kept as small as practical and that small gaps help efficiency and reduce flutter. It describes a method for setting the gap but gives no measured gap size, sealing rule or tolerance in mm or inches for the gap itself.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Gap: the space between the mating halves "should be at a minimum" (qualitative; no number). Place: hinge gap discussion.
- Gap setting: deflect the control surface to the maximum needed travel with the surfaces as close together as possible (procedure, not a dimension). Place: gap-setting paragraph.
- Other inch figures (hinge slot about 1/2 in deep; about 1/16 in of covering removed around each slot; hinges on surfaces as thin as 1/32 in) are not gap values. Place: hinge installation text.
