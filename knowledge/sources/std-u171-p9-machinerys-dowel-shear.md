## Summary
Handbook excerpt reproducing the shear-strength requirement of an American National Standard for unhardened ground dowel pins. It gives minimum single-shear strengths by pin material. The requirement is for dowel pins, not hinge pins.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Single-shear strength, steel unhardened ground dowel pins: 64,000 psi minimum = 441.3 MPa (1 psi = 4.44822 N / (0.0254 m)² = 6894.757 Pa, exact from the stated factors). Bound: minimum. Place: printed p. 1883, "Shear Strength" subsection of "American National Standard Unhardened Ground Dowel Pins". Short quote: "single shear strength of 64,000 psi minimum".
- Single-shear strength, brass unhardened ground dowel pins: 40,000 psi minimum = 275.8 MPa (same conversion). Bound: minimum. Place: same subsection, brass clause. Not used for CT-PIN-HINGE (steel).

## How we could use it
- CT-PIN-HINGE: derivation input for single-shear strength of a steel pin (formula and pin diameter input in the values table).
- Applies to dowel pins to the handbook standard; a printed-knuckle hinge pin needs its own shear basis before use.
