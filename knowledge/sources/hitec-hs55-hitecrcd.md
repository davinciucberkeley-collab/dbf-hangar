Manufacturer product page for the Hitec HS-55 sub-micro analog feather servo. It gives overall size, weight, torque, speed and a shaft label. It does not give mounting ear dimensions, output spline offset, rotation range or lead side. Read via WebFetch; the page's specs image was not readable.

- Weight: 8.0 g (spec table).
