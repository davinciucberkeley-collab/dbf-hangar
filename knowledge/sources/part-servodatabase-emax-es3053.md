## Summary
Database entry for the Emax ES3053, a 17 g digital servo and not the HV model. It gives 28.4 x 13.0 x 31.1 mm, 23T spline and 145 degree rotational range. It is useful only as a reference point for the ES3054 family, because the HV model's dimensions are not confirmed by this page.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Dimensions (L x W x H): L 28.4 mm, W 13.0 mm, H 31.1 mm (specifications section). Model: ES3053.
- Weight: 17.0 g (0.60 oz) (specifications section).
- Spline count: 23 T (specifications section).
- Rotational range: 145° (specifications section).
- Torque: 3.50 kg-cm at 6.0 V; 3.00 kg-cm at 4.8 V (specifications section).
- Mounting ear info: not provided (compatible hardware section lists "Mounting Hardware" only).
- Lead exit side: not stated.

## How we could use it
- Reference only for the 17 g ES3054 family; do not use its H (31.1 mm) for the HV model, which other sources give as about 26.7–28.5 mm.
- Not usable for ES3054HV frames or ports.
