## Summary
The 2026-27 AIAA Design/Build/Fly rules (31st competition, 15-18 April 2027, Tucson) set no transport or shipping box size for the airplane: teams get the airplane to the fly-off by any means they choose. They do set a maximum wingspan, a 5-minute staging-box assembly limit, and a minimum distance from the arming plug to any propeller plane. The team's own transport box (about 1.25 x 0.55 x 0.45 m external, for the one-piece K09 tailsitter) comes from the DBF-Combined config study, not from the rules, and is marked there as an estimate pending car measurement. No wiring clearance value was found in any opened source.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Transport/shipping box size limit for the airplane in the DBF rules: not found. The rules leave transport to the team: "use any other means of transportation" (§2.4 Contest Fly-off Site, p. 9).
- Maximum allowable wingspan 6 ft (1.83 m) (DBF 2026-27 rules §3.1 General, p. 10).
- Staging-box assembly time limit 5 min, covering battery and payload installation, flight-control check and flight preparation (DBF 2026-27 rules §3.3.2 Staging, p. 19).
- Arming plug: no part closer than 6 in (152 mm) to the plane of any propeller (DBF 2026-27 rules §3.2.2 Arming and Safety, p. 14).
- Team transport box (K09-L, one-piece aircraft): one foam-lined box about 1.25 x 0.55 x 0.45 m external, <= 6 kg, one person, field-ready in <= 5 min; previous numbers marked `ASSUMPTION`, car measurement still open (DBF-Combined redesign/config-study/REQUIREMENTS-PROPOSED.md, row P9).
- Same box: about 1.25 x 0.55 x 0.45 m external, ~6 kg, "largest box in the study" (DBF-Combined redesign/config-study/judging/build-and-transport.md, K09 row line 40 and line 95).
- K09 card figure: aircraft 1.15 x 0.45 x 0.39 m with props off; card headline says a 1.2 x 0.5 x 0.4 m box; box mass about 5 kg `ESTIMATE` (DBF-Combined redesign/config-study/cards/K09/CARD.md, lines 50 and 65).
- DECISIONS-ADOPTED.md and inputs/decisions/D1 to D15: no box size found.
- Wiring clearance value (wire to structure/prop/moving part): not found in any opened source.

## How we could use it
- Evidence for D5 and U2.1 task T7 (transport box and wiring clearance); no pending standards key exists for it yet. A future geometry check could hold the envelope to the measured box and the span to <= 6 ft.
- The 6 in arming-plug-to-propeller-plane distance and the 5-min staging limit could feed a regulatory/assembly check if the team targets DBF 2026-27.
