## Summary
Database entry for the EMAX ES9051 4.3 g digital mini servo. It gives overall size, weight, torque and speed at four voltages, rotation range, gear type, connector and wire length. It has no spline teeth, spline offset, ear or drawing values. The page itself says some entries come from community contributions and AI, so treat values as low confidence until checked on a physical unit.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Dimensions (L x W x H): 19.8 mm x 8.9 mm x 23.1 mm (page "Dimensions (L x W x H)").
- Weight: 4.3 g (page "Weight").
- Torque: 1.00 kg-cm at 6.0 V; 0.90 kg-cm at 5.5 V; 0.85 kg-cm at 4.8 V; 0.70 kg-cm at 4.0 V (page "Torque"). The N-m column is the same 0.1 at every voltage, so it is a rounding artifact and was not used.
- Speed (sec/60 deg): 0.06 at 6.0 V; 0.08 at 5.5 V; 0.07 at 4.8 V; 0.10 at 4.0 V (page "Speed").
- Rotation range: 120 deg (page "Rotation range").
- Gear type: nylon (page "Gear type").
- Connector: JR / JST (page "Connector"). Wire length: 180 mm (page "Wire length").
- Not on page: spline teeth, spline position offset, ear height/span/hole spacing, lead exit side.

## How we could use it
- Candidate 4.3 g wing servo envelope (19.8 x 8.9 x 23.1 mm) for the 1:10 wing bay. Confidence low: single database source, community data.
- Torque at 4.8 V (0.85 kg-cm) is a candidate check for wing loads; geometry still needs a drawing.
