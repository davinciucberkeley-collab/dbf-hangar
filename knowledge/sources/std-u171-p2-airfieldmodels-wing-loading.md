## Summary
Model-aircraft website page with the wing loading formula and a worked example. It gives two illustrative sizes but no class ranges or limits. It uses oz/ft^2 only.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [wing_loading_max_g_dm2] No class range or limit stated. Two illustrative figures (8 and 35 oz/ft^2) are given, and the page says they are not meant to be taken literally (section "Why Wing Loading is Important").
- [wing_loading_max_g_dm2] Worked example gives 21.1 oz/ft^2 for a 5.5 lb aircraft with 600 in^2 of wing area (section "How to Calculate Wing Loading"). A calculation example, not a bound.
