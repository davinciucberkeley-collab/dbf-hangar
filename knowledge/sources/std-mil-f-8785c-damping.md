## Summary
A 1986 NASA contractor report reproduces the MIL-F-8785C (August 1969) criteria tables for short-period, Dutch roll and spiral modes. This is a secondary reproduction, not the specification text; the report's own reference list cites MIL-F-8785C, August 1969. Table 2 and Table 6 are legible. The Dutch roll table (Table 4) comes from OCR with a garbled column header, so only the rows that read clearly are recorded. MIL-F-8785C paragraph numbers are not in this text, so table numbers in the report are cited instead.

## Usable rules / numbers
Short-period damping ratio limits, "Table 2: Short period damping ratio limits", report p. 46 (MIL-F-8785C reproduction):
- Level 1, Category A and C flight phases: minimum 0.35, maximum 1.30 (Table 2, p. 46)
- Level 1, Category B flight phases: minimum 0.30, maximum 2.00 (Table 2, p. 46)
- Level 2, Category A and C: minimum 0.25, maximum 2.00 (Table 2, p. 46)
- Level 2, Category B: minimum 0.20, maximum 2.00 (Table 2, p. 46)
- Level 3, Category A and C: minimum 0.15, no maximum (Table 2, p. 46)
- Level 3, Category B: minimum 0.15 (Table 2, p. 46)

Minimum Dutch roll damping and damping-frequency product, "Table 4: Minimum Dutch roll frequency and damping", report p. 49 (OCR; damping-ratio column header not legible; the zeta*omega_n column label is inferred from its rad/s unit):
- Level 1, Category A, class I and IV: minimum damping ratio 0.19; minimum zeta*omega_n 0.35 rad/s (Table 4, p. 49, OCR)
- Level 1, Category A, class II and III: minimum damping ratio 0.19; minimum zeta*omega_n 0.35 rad/s (Table 4, p. 49, OCR)
- Level 1, Category B, all classes: minimum damping ratio 0.08; minimum zeta*omega_n 0.15 rad/s (Table 4, p. 49, OCR)
- Level 1, Category C, class I, II-C and IV: minimum damping ratio 0.08; minimum zeta*omega_n 0.15 rad/s (Table 4, p. 49, OCR)
- Level 1, Category C, class II-L and III: minimum damping ratio 0.08; minimum zeta*omega_n 0.10 rad/s (Table 4, p. 49, OCR)
- Minimum omega_n column for Level 1: OCR column alignment not reliable; not recorded. (Table 4, p. 49, OCR)

Spiral stability, minimum time to double amplitude, "Table 6", report p. 50 (OCR row labels):
- Level 1, Category A and C: 12 s (Table 6, p. 50, OCR)
- Level 1, Category B: 20 s (Table 6, p. 50, OCR)
- Level 2, all categories: 8 s (Table 6, p. 50)
- Level 3, all categories: 4 s (Table 6, p. 50)

Not found in this text: Level 1 phugoid damping; short-period frequency limits (Figures 18 and 19, p. 47-48, graphical only).
