## Summary
Text-layer read of TCDS 3A12 Revision 84 (dated July 29, 2015, p.1), a PDF copy linked from the Univair TCDS page (resources.univair.com/docs/tcds-for-cessna). The 172S entry is on pp.29-31 (Model 172S, Skyhawk SP). Revision 84 is the one read; the current FAA revision was not opened in this run, so the values are not confirmed as current. The FAA RGL host did not answer in this run.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Datum (172S entry): lower portion of front face of firewall. (p.29)
- Empty weight CG range (172S): none. (p.29 prints "None"; the certificated empty weight value itself is not in the sheet.)
- CG range, normal category: aft limit 47.3 in aft of datum at 2,550 lb or less; forward limit 35.0 in aft of datum at 1,950 lb or less, varying linearly to 41.0 in at 2,550 lb. (p.29)
- CG range, utility category: aft limit 40.5 in aft of datum at 2,200 lb or less; forward limit 35.0 in at 1,950 lb or less, varying linearly to 37.5 in at 2,200 lb. (p.29)
- Maximum weights, normal category: maximum ramp 2,558 lb; maximum takeoff and landing 2,550 lb. (p.29)
- Maximum weights, utility category: maximum ramp 2,208 lb; maximum takeoff and landing 2,200 lb. (p.29)
- MAC 58.8 in; leading edge of MAC 25.9 in aft of datum. (p.29)
- Control surface movements (degrees, as printed on p.30): ailerons up 20 +/-1, down 15 +/-1; elevator tab up 22 (+1/-0), down 19 (+1/-0); elevator up 28 (+1/-0), down 23 (+1/-0); rudder measured parallel to waterline 16 deg 10 min +/-1 each side; rudder measured perpendicular to hinge 17 deg 44 min +/-1 each side. (p.30)
- Wing flaps: takeoff 0 to 10 deg; landing 0 to 30 deg (+0/-2). (p.30) No separate trim tab is listed; the elevator tab is the only tab entry.
- Airfoil designation, wing incidence, dihedral: none (not in the sheet text).
- Engine: Lycoming IO-360-L2A, 180 hp, 2,700 rpm maximum for all operations. (p.29)
- Propeller: McCauley 1A170E/JHA7660, diameter not over 76 in and not under 75 in; static rpm at full throttle 2,300 to 2,400. (p.29)
- Certified load factors (172S): none for the normal category in this section. The ferry-flight note (p.31, Note 3) allows +2.5 g to -1.0 g only for the overweight ferry authorization, not as an operating limit.
- Airspeed limits, 172S (p.29): maneuvering 105 KIAS (102 KCAS); max structural cruising 129 KIAS (126 KCAS); never exceed 163 KIAS (160 KCAS); flaps extended 85 KIAS (85 KCAS).
- Fuel and oil (172S, p.29): 56 gal total, 53 gal usable, two wing tanks at 48.0 in aft of datum; oil 8.0 qt at 13.1 in forward of datum.
- Seats and baggage (172S, p.29): 4 seats, two at 34.0 to 46.0 in aft and two at 73.0 in aft; baggage 120 lb at 82.0 to 108.0 in and 50 lb at 108.0 to 142.0 in (combined max 120 lb).
- Unusable fuel and oil in the certificated empty weight (172S, p.31, Note 1): 18 lb of unusable fuel at 46.0 in aft; full oil 15.0 lb at 13.1 in forward.
- Leveling means (172S, p.29): left side of tailcone at 108.0 in and 142.0 in aft of datum.
