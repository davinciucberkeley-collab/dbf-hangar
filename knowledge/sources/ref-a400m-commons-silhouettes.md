## Summary
A 600 x 1000 px black silhouette of the A400M in front, side and top view, licence CC BY-SA 3.0 (Commons file page metadata; attribution to the author required, share-alike). Downloaded through the fetch tool (a direct curl got an HTTP error page from Wikimedia) and measured by pixel scan. The silhouette is internally consistent: the scale from the span and from the length differs by 0.05%.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Scale 0.0788 m/px from span 42.4 m = 538 px (top view) and 45.1 m = 572 px (side view).
- Cross-check against published values: wing area -3.0%, height +1.9%, length +0.8%, prop diameter -1.9% (see reference-aircraft/a400m.md section 7b).
- Geometry values taken: wing chords and sweep, anhedral about 6 deg, tail geometry, engine positions, fuselage stations, gear positions (see that sheet).
- Silhouette only: no inner lines, so no control-surface or airfoil data.
