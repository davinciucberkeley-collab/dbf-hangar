## Summary
Text-layer read of a Cessna 172S handbook PDF hosted by befa.org. Its front matter states Revision 5 (19 July 2004); some section footers read Revision 4, July 8/98 or May 30/00, and the notice says it is not kept current and is not a substitute for the operating handbook. It is therefore not confirmed as the current Textron revision; the numbers below are the ones printed in it. Page references are PDF page numbers, with the printed page label in brackets where useful. The three-view dimensions are figure labels only, and the overall length and height are not in the text layer.

## Usable rules / numbers
Section 1 (General)
- Wing area 174 sq ft. (PDF p.11, note 4; printed 1-3)
- Wing span 36 ft 1 in (dimension on the three-view). (PDF p.10; printed 1-2)
- Wheelbase 65 in; minimum turning radius 27 ft 5.5 in (pivot to outboard wingtip). (PDF p.11, notes 2 and 5)
- Wing loading 14.7 lb/sq ft; power loading 14.2 lb/hp. (PDF p.15, printed 1-7; also PDF p.3)
- Overall length and overall height: not found (figure text not in the text layer).

Section 2 (Limitations)
- VNE 163 KIAS (160 KCAS); VNO 129 KIAS (126 KCAS). (PDF p.40, Figure 2-1; printed 2-4)
- VA 105 KIAS (102 KCAS) at 2,550 lb; 98 KIAS (95 KCAS) at 2,200 lb; 90 KIAS (88 KCAS) at 1,900 lb. (PDF p.40)
- VFE 110 KIAS (107 KCAS) for 0 to 10 deg flaps; 85 KIAS (85 KCAS) for 10 to 30 deg flaps. (PDF p.40)
- Vs1 48 KIAS: lower limit of the white arc, flaps retracted at most forward CG. (PDF p.41; printed 2-5)
- Vs0 40 KIAS: lower limit of the green arc, landing configuration. (PDF p.41; printed 2-5)
- Vx: value not found (defined on PDF p.16; no number printed in the text layer).
- Vy: best rate-of-climb speed 74 KIAS at sea level and 72 KIAS at 10,000 ft. (PDF p.81, Section 4)
- Weight limits, normal category: ramp 2,558 lb; takeoff 2,550 lb; landing 2,550 lb. Utility category: ramp 2,208 lb; takeoff 2,200 lb; landing 2,200 lb. (PDF p.43; printed 2-7)
- CG envelope, normal category: forward 35.0 in aft of datum at 1,950 lb or less, straight line to 41.0 in at 2,550 lb; aft 47.3 in aft of datum at all weights. Datum: lower portion of front face of firewall. (PDF p.43; printed 2-7)
- CG envelope, utility category: forward 35.0 in at 1,950 lb or less, straight line to 37.5 in at 2,200 lb; aft 40.5 in at all weights. (PDF p.44)
- Flight load factors, normal category at 2,550 lb: flaps up +3.8 g and -1.52 g; flaps down +3.0 g. Utility at 2,200 lb: flaps up +4.4 g and -1.76 g; flaps down +3.0 g. (PDF p.46; printed 2-10)
- Flap travel: takeoff 0 to 10 deg (PDF p.101, Section 4; deflection above 10 deg not approved for takeoff); landing range 0 to 30 deg as used in the checklist (PDF p.93).

Section 4 and 5 (stall, climb, cruise, distance; conditions as printed)
- Stall speeds, power off, 2,550 lb, most rearward CG, KIAS/KCAS at bank 0 deg: flaps up 48/53; flaps 10 deg 42/50; flaps 30 deg 40/48. (PDF p.124; printed 5-12, Figure 5-3). Same table at 30, 45 and 60 deg bank: flaps up 52/57, 57/63, 68/75; flaps 10 deg 45/54, 50/59, 59/71; flaps 30 deg 43/52, 48/57, 57/68.
- Stall speeds, most forward CG, bank 0 deg: flaps up 48/53; flaps 10 deg 43/51; flaps 30 deg 40/48. (PDF p.124)
- Flaps-up power-off stall speed 53 KCAS and flaps-down 48 KCAS, at 2,550 lb. (PDF p.2, Performance Specifications)
- Rate of climb at sea level: 730 fpm (spec page; weights at 2,550 lb, standard atmospheric conditions). (PDF p.2 and p.3 note)
- Maximum rate of climb, 2,550 lb, flaps up, full throttle, 74 KIAS at sea level: 855 fpm at -20 C, 785 fpm at 0 C, 710 fpm at 20 C, 645 fpm at 40 C. (PDF p.129, Figure 5-6; printed 5-17. Row alignment read from layout text; consistent with the 730 fpm spec figure.)
- Cruise, 75 percent power at 8,500 ft: 124 KTAS; range 518 NM, 4.26 h, 53 gal usable. (PDF p.2)
- Cruise table, 2,550 lb, 8,000 ft, standard temperature: 77 percent BHP, 124 KTAS, 10.4 GPH on the row shown at 2,400 RPM. (PDF p.132, Figure 5-8 sheet 2; printed 5-20. The RPM label alignment in the text layer is uncertain for this block.)
- Takeoff distance, 2,550 lb, standard atmosphere, level, hard dry surface, no wind: ground roll 960 ft; total over 50 ft obstacle 1,630 ft. (PDF p.2; conditions PDF p.3)
- Landing distance, same conditions: ground roll 575 ft; total over 50 ft obstacle 1,335 ft. (PDF p.2; conditions PDF p.3)

Section 6 (Weight and balance)
- Standard empty weight 1,663 lb. (PDF p.15, printed 1-7; PDF p.2)
- Sample loading problem: basic empty weight 1,642 lb with moment 62.6 (lb-in /1000); usable fuel 180 lb (30 gal) at moment 8.6; front pilot and passenger 340 lb at 12.6; rear passengers 340 lb at 24.8; baggage area 2 of 56 lb at 4.6; ramp 2,558 lb at 113.2; takeoff 2,550 lb at 112.8. (PDF p.148, Figure 6-5 sheet 1; printed 6-12)
- Empty CG arm: not printed. Derived from the sample line as 62.6 / 1,642, about 38.1 in aft of datum (derived, not a printed value).
- Fuel station arm: not printed. Derived from the sample as 8.6 / 180, about 47.8 in (derived). Fuel tanks at 48.0 in aft per the TCDS.
- Pilot and front passenger station 34 to 46 in; sample arm derived as 12.6 / 340, about 37.1 in (derived). Rear passenger station 73 in per the TCDS; sample derived 72.9 in (derived).
- Sample takeoff CG: 112.8 / 2,550, about 44.2 in aft of datum (derived); the handbook states this point is inside the envelope.
- Mean aerodynamic chord: 58.80 in on the weighing form (PDF p.140); leading edge at 25.90 in aft of datum in the CG-percent-MAC formula (PDF p.141).

## How we could use it
- Supplies the 172S performance and CG-envelope figures for the reference-aircraft calibration (stall, climb, cruise, distance and envelope checks); because it is Rev 5 (2004), compare each value against the current Textron handbook before using it as a gate.
- figures still missing: Vx number; overall length and height as numeric values; the printed empty-weight CG arm (only derived values from the sample loading); 172S airfoil, incidence and dihedral (not in this handbook text); current Textron revision. The stall table row for 10 deg flaps is printed as "100" in the text layer and is read here as 10 deg.
