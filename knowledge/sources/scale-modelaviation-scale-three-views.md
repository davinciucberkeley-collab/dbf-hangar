## Summary
Scale-competition advice on documenting a prototype before building: collect photos first, then check every three-view drawing against them, because factory and published drawings are often inaccurate and production aircraft changed in service. It does not discuss enlarging tails, wing area or incidence.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Gather photos of the full-size aircraft first and check three-views against them (opening advice).
- Do not trust a factory drawing by default; several drawings of one type (at least four for the PT-19) disagree (drawing-accuracy paragraphs).
- Allow for production changes and field modifications (P-47 example).
- Record each deviation from the drawing (modeler example).

## How we could use it
- Template: every geometry field carries a source and a `confidence` (measured | drawing | photo | estimate); two sources preferred for span, length, wing area.
