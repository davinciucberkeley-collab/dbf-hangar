## Summary
Manufacturer index of EMAX servos. It lists ES3054 and ES3054HV by name but gives no dimensions, mounting ear data, travel or spline position for either. Its ES3054 entry gives 17 g, 3.5 kg and 0.13 s, and its ES3054HV entry gives no numeric values.

## Usable rules / numbers
- ES3054: weight 17 g, torque 3.5 kg, speed 0.13 s; described as metal-gear version of a plastic-gear twin (servo table).
- ES3054HV: listed as a high-voltage servo; no weight, torque, speed, spline, dimensions, travel or mounting data (servo table).
- Other models in the table give dimensions, travel and mounting data: none (no model has them).

## How we could use it
- Confirms EMAX lists the ES3054HV name but gives no geometry; the manufacturer source for frames/ports is still missing.
- Not usable for frames or ports.
