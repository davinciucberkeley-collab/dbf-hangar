## Summary
Distributor catalogue page listing threaded clevis rod ends with a tensile-strength column for each material. These are threaded rod-end clevises, not pushrod wire ends in a horn. The page gives material tensile strength only, with no bound type and no joint load.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Zinc-plated carbon steel clevis rod ends: 58,000 psi tensile strength = 399.9 MPa (1 psi = 6894.757 Pa, exact from the stated factors). Bound type not stated. Sizes 10-32 to 3/4-16 (6 1/16 in length); several sizes listed "Not Rated". Place: "Clevis Rod Ends, zinc-plated carbon steel", Tensile Strength column.
- Corrosion-resistant 316 stainless clevis rod ends: 80,000 psi = 551.6 MPa (same factor). Bound type not stated. Sizes 10-24 to 1-12 and M5 to M16. Place: "Corrosion-Resistant Clevis Rod Ends, 316 stainless steel", Tensile Strength column.
- No 4-40 or M2 size appears in the rows read.

## How we could use it
- CT-CLEVIS: material tensile input for a threaded steel clevis only. It is not a pushrod-end joint rating, and it does not set the joint value.
- A pushrod-wire tensile table (ASTM A228 by diameter) or a clevis maker's joint rating is still needed for a pushrod end.
