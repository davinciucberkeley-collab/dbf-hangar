## Summary
Manufacturer product page for the current Cessna Skyhawk piston, with a printed specification table (dimensions, weights, engine, fuel, performance). The page does not state a model year; the reference sheet takes its engine and weight values as the 172S values, the same reading the AOPA fact sheet gives. Performance figures are manufacturer calculations at standard conditions.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Length 27 ft 2 in (8.3 m); height 8 ft 11 in (2.7 m); wingspan 36 ft 1 in (11.00 m); wing area 174 sq ft (16.17 sq m). (specification table)
- Basic empty weight 1,680 lb (762 kg); maximum takeoff weight 2,550 lb (1,157 kg); maximum ramp weight 2,558 lb (1,160 kg); useful load 878 lb (398 kg). (specification table)
- Engine Lycoming IO-360-L2A, 180 hp; usable fuel 53 gal (201 l), 318 lb (144 kg). (specification table)
- Maximum cruise 124 ktas; stall 48 kcas; service ceiling 14,000 ft; maximum range 640 nm; maximum climb 730 fpm; 4 occupants. (specification table)
- Takeoff distance 1,630 ft (497 m) and landing distance 1,335 ft (407 m); the page does not print the obstacle height. (specification table)
