## Summary
Pilot's Operating Handbook limitations page for the 172R (revision dated December 1996), read from a third-party hosted copy. It gives normal-category weights, baggage limits and the forward and aft centre-of-gravity limits in inches aft of the firewall datum. It does not cover the 172S and gives no gear track or wheelbase.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Normal category: maximum ramp 2,457 lb; maximum takeoff and maximum landing 2,450 lb. (page 2-7, normal category)
- Forward CG limit 35.0 in aft of datum at 1,950 lb or less, sloping straight to 40.0 in at 2,450 lb. (page 2-7)
- Aft CG limit 47.3 in aft of datum at all weights. (page 2-7)
- Datum: lower portion of the front face of the firewall. (page 2-7)
- Baggage area 1 (station 82 to 108) 120 lb; area 2 (station 108 to 142) 50 lb; combined limit 120 lb. (page 2-7)
- Utility category: maximum takeoff 2,100 lb; CG limits not on this page. (page 2-7)

## How we could use it
- Source for the CG envelope in section 6 of the reference sheet, as 172R values until a 172S POH is checked.
- Basis for the static-margin and CG placement check in section 7.
