## Summary
Opened on 2026-11-25 (same URL as std-ct-fasteners). The page gives minimum breaking torques for metric steel screws in classes 8.8 and 12.9, including M2.5, M5 and M6. It gives no tensile stress area, proof load or minimum tensile load for M2.5, M5 or M6; its size-independent table gives test stress in MPa only.

## Usable rules / numbers
- M2.5 (pitch 0.45): minimum breaking torque 0.82 Nm (class 8.8) and 1.1 Nm (class 12.9) (table "Breaking torque ISO 898-7 DIN 267 pt25", column "Breaking torque (Nm) minimum"). Torque, not force.
- M5 (pitch 0.8): minimum breaking torque 7.6 Nm (8.8) and 10 Nm (12.9) (same table).
- M6 (pitch 1): minimum breaking torque 13 Nm (8.8) and 17 Nm (12.9) (same table).
- M2.5 tensile stress area, proof load and minimum tensile load: `not found` on this page.
- M5 and M6 tensile stress area, proof load and minimum tensile load: `not found` on this page (see std-u171-p8-engtoolbox-bolt-loads for the force values).

## How we could use it
- Torque-only evidence for the M2.5 screws in CT-PROP-ADAPTER and CT-A-M25-SHCS-MOTOR. It does not rate a joint in N.
- Breaking torque for M5 and M6 studs and nuts in CT-A-PROP-NUT and CT-PROP-NUT; not a clamp torque.
