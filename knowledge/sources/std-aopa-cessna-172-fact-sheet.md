## Summary
Pilot-association fact sheet for the Cessna 172 with a summary spec table. It repeats the length, height, span, wing area and useful load of the manufacturer page, and gives stall speeds with and without flaps. It does not state a variant for each figure.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Length 27 ft 2 in; height 8 ft 11 in; wingspan 36 ft 1 in; wing area 174 sq ft. (spec table)
- Empty weight 1,680 lb; "maximum gross weight" 2,558 lb (equals the manufacturer's ramp figure, not takeoff); useful load 878 lb. (spec table)
- Engine Lycoming IO-360-L2A, 180 hp; fuel 56 gal (53 gal usable). (spec table)
- Stall VS1 48 KIAS, VSO 40 KIAS; cruise 124 kt at 75% power; service ceiling 14,000 ft; range 640 nm; climb 730 fpm at sea level. (spec table)
- Seat count field is blank on the page. (spec table)

## How we could use it
- Second source for span, length, height and wing area (two-source rule in the reference template).
- Source for the flaps-down stall speed (VSO) used in the stall check of section 7.
