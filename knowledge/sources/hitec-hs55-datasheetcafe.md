Third-party datasheet aggregator page for the Hitec HS-55. Gives overall size in mm and inches, weight, torque and speed for 4.8 V and 6.0 V, and motor type. Mount ears, spline offset, rotation range and lead side are not stated. The linked hs-55.pdf and pinout image were not readable through this fetch.
