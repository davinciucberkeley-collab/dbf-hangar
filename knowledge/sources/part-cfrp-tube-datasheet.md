## Summary
Two pultruded carbon tube sources were read in this run. The Goodwinds sheet, distributed by Aircraft Spruce, gives minimum tensile and flexural properties for carbon tube and no tube diameter range. The Rock West listing gives outer and inner diameters for 30 round unidirectional tubes but no strength, modulus or test standard. Neither source names an ASTM test method for the tube values.

## Usable rules / numbers
Goodwinds, "Carbon tube minimum properties" block (PDF p. 1; block alignment rebuilt from the layout order, check on the vendor page):
- Product: pultruded carbon tube, matrix Bisphenol epoxy vinyl ester; fiber volume 60% (Goodwinds sheet, carbon tube block, p. 1)
- Tensile strength (axial, minimum): 240 ksi = 1.65 GPa (Goodwinds sheet, carbon tube block, p. 1)
- Tensile modulus (axial, minimum): 19.5 msi = 134 GPa (Goodwinds sheet, carbon tube block, p. 1)
- Flexural strength (minimum): 200 ksi = 1.37 GPa (Goodwinds sheet, carbon tube block, p. 1)
- Flexural modulus (minimum): 18.5 msi = 127 GPa (Goodwinds sheet, carbon tube block, p. 1)
- Ultimate tensile strain: 1.40% (Goodwinds sheet, carbon tube block, p. 1)
- Test standard for these values: not stated (Goodwinds sheet, p. 1)
- Tube outer diameter range: not stated in the Goodwinds sheet (rod table lists 0.019 in to 0.156 in rods only, p. 1)
Rock West Composites listing (WebFetch summary of the product table, units not printed in the table; part-number pattern suggests inches):
- Outer diameter range in listing: 0.039 to 0.626 (table column "Outside Dimension"; Rock West listing, product table, web page: no page numbers)
- Inner diameter range: 0.020 to 0.480; wall 0.010 to 0.0728 (Rock West listing, product table)
- Fiber direction: "Unidirectional" for all 30 items (Rock West listing, product table)
- Strength, modulus, test standard: not stated on the Rock West page (Rock West listing, product table)

## How we could use it
- Feeds U18.3 as a minimum-property candidate for carbon tube spars: 240 ksi tensile and 19.5 msi modulus, with the flexural values as a separate basis; treat as minimum, not design allowable, until a datasheet with an ASTM method is read.
- Tube OD choice for the spar scoping: Rock West diameters, combined with the Goodwinds properties (different makers; not one product).
- values still missing: test standard (ASTM D3039 or other) and fiber-direction statement for the Goodwinds tube block; compressive strength; a single maker sheet giving both OD range and strength with the test method.
