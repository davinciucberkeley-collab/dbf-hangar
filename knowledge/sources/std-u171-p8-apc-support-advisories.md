## Summary
Opened on 2026-11-25. APC's advisories page covers multirotor hub types and gives fastener sizes for one hub and a hand-tighten instruction for a nut. None of the advisories gives a torque or clamp value for a hub or spinner nut. The companion quick-release PDF could not be read as text.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Quick-Release Hubs for Multi-Rotor Propellers (June 1, 2022): the hub attaches to the motor housing with two 3 mm screws at 12 mm spacing; no torque or clamp force stated (advisory body).
- Self-Tightening MR Propellers (May 1, 2015): the propellers include an M6x1 hex nut that fits into the hub; installation is to spin the propeller onto the shaft and hand tighten; no torque or clamp force stated (advisory body).
- Centering Propellers on Motor Shaft: mentions adapter rings; no installation torque or clamp value (advisory body).
- Hub or nut torque for the APC prop nut on an M5 stud: `not found`.

## How we could use it
- Source for the fastener sizes of an APC quick-release hub (2 x M3 at 12 mm). That hub is not the T-Motor adapter in CT-A-PROP-ADAPTER, so it is context only.
- Source for the M6x1 nut and hand-tighten method; it is the only APC nut evidence for CT-PROP-NUT. No joint torque.
