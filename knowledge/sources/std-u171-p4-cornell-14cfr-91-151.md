## Summary
This regulation sets a fuel reserve for airplanes flying under VFR. It is written as a flight-time reserve after reaching the first intended landing point, not as a fraction of energy. It applies to fuel, not to batteries. It gives no battery or electric reserve percentage.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [energy_reserve_fraction, time rule only] Day VFR airplane: fly at least 30 minutes more after reaching the first intended landing point, at normal cruising speed (minimum, minutes). Place: section "Fuel requirements for flight in VFR conditions", paragraph (a)(1).
- [energy_reserve_fraction, time rule only] Night VFR airplane: at least 45 minutes (minimum, minutes). Place: same section, paragraph (a)(2).

## How we could use it
- Does not feed `energy_reserve_fraction` as a fraction; it is a fuel time rule and could only inform a time-based reserve if the design chose that form.
