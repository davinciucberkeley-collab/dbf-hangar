## Summary
A 1202 x 1751 px dimensioned line drawing from the maker, public domain per the Commons file page. It shows an early straight-tail 172 (printed 36 ft span, 24 ft length, 8 ft 6 in height, 7 ft 2 in track, tailplane 10 ft 8 in). The three views are at different scales and the side view is rotated 4.65 deg (5 deg printed). Fetched through the fetch tool and measured by pixel scan.

## Usable rules / numbers
- Scales: top 0.01002 m/px, front about 0.0096, side 0.00817 m/px (each from its own printed dimension).
- Cross-check vs published 172S: wing area -0.2%, span -0.3%, height 0.0% (side, de-rotated), length -8% (variant difference); front-view height +11%.
