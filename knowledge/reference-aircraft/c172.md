# Reference aircraft fact sheet: Cessna 172 Skyhawk (172S; 172R where marked)

Rules: full-size values; lengths in metres, areas m2, masses kg, speeds m/s, angles degrees. The model scale factor is applied in section 7. Every row needs a value, a source (id or URL you opened) and a confidence (measured, drawing, photo, estimate). Write `unknown` rather than guess. Get two sources for span, length and wing area (done for span, length and wing area; see section 1 and 2). Save the filled sheet as `knowledge/reference-aircraft/<kebab-id>.md`.

Variant note: the Textron page and the AOPA fact sheet are the 172S values as printed (neither gives a model year on each figure). Values marked "172R" come from the 172R POH page or Wikipedia's 172R block and are not 172S values. Source ids are the `knowledge/sources/` filenames. Confidence "published" means a printed manufacturer or POH value that was opened; no drawing or photo was checked in this run.

## 8. Gaps
- Three-view and photo links: no three-view drawing was opened (all geometry in sections 1 to 3 and 5 is unknown).
- Root and tip chord, sweep, dihedral, twist, incidence, airfoil (lead: NACA 2412 modified from an unopened listing): need a checked three-view and an airfoil source.
- Fuselage stations, cross-section shapes, nose and tail-cone shape: need a three-view and cross-section photos.
- Tail layout and all horizontal and vertical tail dimensions: need a three-view. Tail volumes in section 7 cannot be computed.
- Engine position, thrust line, cowl and nacelle dimensions, spinner: need a three-view and photos.
- Propeller for the 172S (printed value opened is the 172R McCauley 1C235/LFA7570, 1.91 m): confirm in the 172S POH or TCDS.
- Strut count and attachment; landing gear arrangement, wheel size, track, wheelbase: need the TCDS or a dimensioned three-view.
- CG envelope is the 172R POH; the 172S envelope and the % MAC conversion (needs MAC from a three-view) are not verified.
- Model year of the 172S and its TCDS revision: the FAA TCDS 3A12 was not read (the BCAA and NZ copies were image-based or unreadable).
- Paint scheme and markings: photo look only, not done.
- Obstacle height for the takeoff and landing distances on the Textron page: not printed.
