# Reference aircraft fact sheet: Cessna 172 Skyhawk (172S; 172R where marked)

Rules: full-size values; lengths in metres, areas m2, masses kg, speeds m/s, angles degrees. The model scale factor is applied in section 7. Every row needs a value, a source (id or URL you opened) and a confidence (measured, drawing, photo, estimate). Write `unknown` rather than guess. Get two sources for span, length and wing area (done for span, length and wing area; see section 1 and 2). Save the filled sheet as `knowledge/reference-aircraft/<kebab-id>.md`.

`drawing-measured` = measured by us on the Commons maker three-view (source ref-c172-commons-3view; early 172, see section 7b). Variant note: the Textron page and the AOPA fact sheet are the 172S values as printed (neither gives a model year on each figure). Values marked "172R" come from the 172R POH page or Wikipedia's 172R block and are not 172S values. Source ids are the `knowledge/sources/` filenames. Confidence "published" means a printed manufacturer or POH value that was opened; `drawing-measured` values come from the three-view only (no photo was checked); `derived` = computed from rows above.

## 8. Gaps
- Three-view: the Commons maker drawing is an early 172, not the 172S; length is 8% shorter and the fin shape differs. A 172R/S drawing (File:1996 Cessna 172R.svg, CC BY-SA 4.0, 591 x 219 px) exists; not measured.
- Twist, incidence, airfoil (lead: NACA 2412 modified from an unopened listing): need an airfoil source. Chords, sweep, dihedral are measured.
- Fuselage stations are measured; cross-section shapes need cross-section photos.
- Tail dimensions are measured on the early-172 drawing (straight tail); the 172S fin is swept, so Vv and fin shape need a 172S drawing.
- Thrust line and spinner diameter: need photos. Engine position, cowl size measured.
- Propeller for the 172S (printed value opened is the 172R McCauley 1C235/LFA7570, 1.91 m): confirm in the 172S POH or TCDS.
- Strut fore-aft position and wheel sizes (+-10%): need a photo. Strut count and attach points, gear arrangement, track, wheelbase measured.
- CG envelope is the 172R POH; the 172S envelope and the % MAC conversion (needs MAC from a three-view) are not verified.
- Model year of the 172S and its TCDS revision: the FAA TCDS 3A12 was not read (the BCAA and NZ copies were image-based or unreadable).
- Paint scheme and markings: photo look only, not done.
- Obstacle height for the takeoff and landing distances on the Textron page: not printed.
