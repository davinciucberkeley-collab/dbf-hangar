# Reference aircraft fact sheet: Airbus A400M Atlas (A400M-180, production)

Rules: full-size values; lengths in metres, areas m2, masses kg, speeds m/s, angles degrees. The model scale factor is applied in section 7. Every row needs a value, a source (id or URL you opened) and a confidence (measured, drawing, photo, estimate). Write `unknown` rather than guess.

Confidence key used here: `published` = published manufacturer or government figure, not measured by us; `estimate` = inferred from category labels or text; `derived` = computed, formula given. `drawing-measured` = measured by us on the Commons silhouette three-view (source ref-a400m-commons-silhouettes), scale 0.0788 m/px from the span (top view) and length (side view). No `photo` value was taken.

## 7b. Three-view measurement cross-check
Scale 0.0788 m/px: top-view span 538 px for 42.4 m; side-view length 572 px for 45.1 m gives 0.07885 m/px (0.05% apart, so the drawing is internally consistent). Pixel step 0.08 m.
| Check | Measured | Published | Error |
|---|---|---|---|
| wing area (trapezoid to centre line) | 218.4 m2 | 225.1 m2 (ref-a400m-wikipedia) | -3.0% |
| overall height (ground to fin top, side and front agree) | 14.98 m | 14.7 m | +1.9% |
| overall length, top view (nose to tailplane tip) | 45.5 m | 45.1 m | +0.8% |
| prop diameter (top view, outer prop) | 5.2 m | 5.3 m | -1.9% |
Not measurable from a silhouette: airfoil, twist, incidence, control-surface spans, hinge lines, wheel size, nacelle length behind the wing LE, thrust-line angles, spinner. Error to expect on lengths +-2%, sweeps +-1.5 deg, dihedral +-2 deg.

## 8. Gaps
Fields left `unknown`, and what to read or measure next:
- Wing: twist, airfoil, incidence, flap and aileron spans (chords, sweep, dihedral, position now measured from the Commons silhouette). Next: photos for dihedral (the drawing shows about -6 deg) and control surfaces.
- Wing area: one source only (225.1 m2). A second source is needed; the search snippet 221.5 m2 conflicts and was not opened.
- Fuselage: stations measured at 10 points; fin-root and tail-end sections not measurable; cross-section photos for true section shape.
- Tail: elevator and rudder chord fractions (geometry measured).
- Propulsion: thrust line, nacelle length and height, spinner size, per-engine rotation (positions measured).
- Landing gear: wheel diameter and width, strut length (positions, track, wheelbase measured).
- Mass and performance: stall speed, CG range, paint scheme. Next: TCDS (readable copy) or flight manual; photos.
- Blocked or unreadable: the OCCAR A400M datasheet returned HTTP 403 (blocked: occar.int); globalmilitary.net returned HTTP 403 (blocked). The EASA data sheet PDF (ref-a400m-easa-tcds) was fetched but returned as compressed binary; no values taken from it. A search snippet quoted EASA values (span 42.357 m, length 45.091 m, height 14.675 m) that were not opened; they are consistent with the values above but are not used as sources.
- Scope note: the A400M is a military type; its TCDS is not confirmed to exist in EASA form. Verify before citing as TCDS.
