## What is wrong today
Control surfaces are a flat cut at `hinge_frac` (`wing.py:134-143`, `tails.py:19-35`, `parts.py::_fin_build`): the moving part's front face is coplanar with the fixed part's back face. Rotating by delta about the hinge line pushes the front corner on one side a distance `a * sin(delta)` into the fixed part (`a` = half the thickness at the hinge; NACA 0012, chord 100, hinge 0.7: a = 3.6634 mm, so 1.548 mm at 25 deg) and every sweep over a few degrees clips. U15.1 aligned the axis with the hinge line (`hinge_from`) but not the geometry.

## Decision (default taken; all opt-in, defaults byte-identical)
Six new generator params on `wing_panel` (part elevon), `tail_surfaces` (part elevator and rudder) and `fin` (part rear):
`hinge_gap` (number >= 0, default 0.0, mm, measured normal to the hinge line, in the horizontal plane), `hinge_style` (choice `none` | `bevel`, default `none`), `hinge_throw_deg` (number >= 0, default 0.0, the largest deflection to clear), `hinge_knuckles` (int >= 0, default 0), `knuckle_d` (number >= 0, default 0.0, mm), `knuckle_len` (number >= 0, default 0.0, mm).
- Gap: the moving part's front face moves aft by `k * hinge_gap` in the section plane, where `k = 1 / cos(L)` and `L` is the sweep of the hinge line (`k = hypot(dx, dy) / |dy|` from the two hinge points at the elevon span ends; fin: `hypot(dx, dz) / |dz|`). The hinge axis, `hinge_line()` and any `hinge_from` pivot stay exactly where they were (on the `hinge_frac` line at mid-thickness); `hinge_line` is NOT edited.
- Bevel: the front face becomes a V ridge on the hinge axis height (the point `(hinge + k*gap, mid-thickness)`) with two chamfer planes running back to the upper and lower skin at `k * (gap + a * tan(throw))` behind the hinge line, where `a` = half the thickness of the moving part at the hinge. Condition it satisfies for every point of the section: `dx >= k * (gap + |dz| * tan(throw))`, so a rotation of up to `throw` never moves any point forward of the fixed part's hinge plane (the extra gap is not needed for clearance, it only keeps the ridge off the plane). The upper and lower skin points beyond the chamfer follow the airfoil: the chord stations are remapped linearly from `[hinge, 1]` to `[hinge + front/c, 1]` (`c` = local chord), the z values are interpolated from the airfoil at the remapped stations, and for `bevel` the ridge point is inserted between the last upper and the first lower point. Point count is identical at every station (the loft needs it): `_unit_loop` points + 1 (bevel) or + 0.
- Knuckles (printed geometry, counts for mass and T1): `hinge_knuckles` cylinders of diameter `knuckle_d`, length `knuckle_len`, axis parallel to the hinge line, centres at `p0 + (p1 - p0) * (i + 0.5) / N + n * hinge_gap` (`p0`, `p1` = the two hinge points at the elevon span ends of that side, `n` = the unit aft normal of the hinge plane), united with the moving part. The knuckle sits on the ridge, so it needs `knuckle_d <= 2 * hinge_gap * cos(throw)` (checked) and the gap in front of it.
- Not in S6 (state in the PR): `hinge_style: round` (needs a matching socket in the FIXED part: a different generator output); fixed-part geometry; `hinge_from` for `tail_surfaces` / `fin` (the tail axis is still hand-typed: on c172-rc the elevator hinge line is swept 3.2 deg against the typed axis `[0,-1,0]`, which costs about 0.5 mm of clearance at 25 deg at the far end; Part B absorbs it with the gap scan below); dihedral tilt of the hinge plane (cos 1.6 deg = 0.9996, ignored).
