## Intent
Opt-in params on the moving part of `wing_panel` (elevon) and `tail_surfaces` (elevator, rudder): `hinge_gap` (mm, default 0), `hinge_style` (`bevel` | `round`, default unset), knuckle count and size at N stations (printed geometry that counts for mass and T1). Bevel or round the surface's leading edge from its articulation range so the swept surface clears the fixed part.
## Acceptance (to be made exact)
Scratch c172-rc full-range T1 pose sweep PASS on the hinges without `embed_ok`; every default (no params) byte-identical on every design (export hashes); U15.1's `hinge_line` still lands on the hinge axis.
