## What is wrong today (measured by W34-A, repeated by W34-T5)
`core/hangar/geometry.py::pose_transforms` rotates everything downstream of a joint rigidly. The pushrod is downstream of the servo arm (`kinematic_link`), the horn pin downstream of the surface hinge, so over a sweep the rod-end-to-pin distance changes (c172-rc elevator 23.8 mm, ailerons 13.6 / 15.4, rudder 13.6): the rod leaves the horn in Studio. `simulate_mechanism` (`core/hangar/gates/mechanisms.py:35`) takes the follower as `clamp(declared ratio x input)`, and T6.link says "not checked (pinned by declared link)".

## Decision (default taken)
- `mechanisms[].kinematics`: `"ratio"` (default, = today's code path, byte-identical) or `"four_bar"`.
- Four-bar geometry, all in the body frame at rest. `tip0` = `location` of the attachment named by `link.driver_end` (the arm tip where the rod is pinned); `pin0` = `location` of `link.follower_end` (the pin in the horn); `L = |pin0 - tip0|`. The driver joint (`mechanism.driver`, articulation `axis`/`pivot`) rotates the tip: `tip(a) = R_driver(a) tip0`. The follower joint (the one attachment in `mechanism.driven`) rotates the pin: `pin(t) = R_follower(t) pin0`. The solve finds `t` with `|pin(t) - tip(a)| = L`, `t` inside the follower's `range_deg`, continuous from `t = 0` at `a = 0`. Works for non-parallel axes (3-D).
- Rod pose: the rigid transform that maps `tip0 -> tip(a)` and turns the unit vector `u0 = (pin0 - tip0)/L` to `u1 = (pin(t) - tip(a))/L` by the minimum rotation (axis `u0 x u1`, angle `atan2(|u0 x u1|, u0 . u1)`, identity when parallel). It replaces the transform of the rod component and of every component downstream of the `driver_end` attachment (`downstream_ids(design, driver_end_attachment)`); no other component changes.
- Jam (T6.mechanism FAIL, never a silent clamp): no root of the follower angle inside `range_deg`; or the transmission angle below `four_bar_min_transmission_deg`; or the follower moves more than `four_bar_max_follower_step_deg` for one degree of input (branch flip). Both thresholds are new, in `standards/gate-thresholds.yaml`, `source: pending` (no published source opened).
- Transmission angle `mu` (deg): angle between the rod vector `pin - tip` and `r`, the part of `pin - follower_pivot` perpendicular to the follower axis, folded into 0..90 (`acos(abs(cos))`). 90 = rod tangent to the horn arc; 0 = dead point.
- T6.link with `four_bar`: PASS row (rod, horn, pin, surface and skin are checked by the existing step collision loop, with `linked = {}`); T6.ratio unchanged.
- Out of scope (state in the PR): T1 pose sweeps still pose the rod rigidly and keep their `T1.kinematic_link` SKIP rows (their baselines do not change); nested motion (a servo or a hinge parent that itself moves with another articulated joint) is refused with a FAIL row.
