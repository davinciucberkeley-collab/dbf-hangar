# Brief U13.4-2-servo-undriven-warn: T6 WARN "servo is not the driver of any mechanism"

class: hard · role: builder-plus · plan: §7 U13.4 item (3) · depends on: U13.4-1 merged (uses its fixture) · merge order: 2nd · SLUG = `servo-undriven`

Environment: follow `plans/2026-11-20-standardization/briefs/U13.4-env.md` with SLUG=servo-undriven.

## Where
Branch `std/u4.39-servo-undriven`. Files you may change: `core/hangar/gates/mechanisms.py` (only `tier6_mechanisms` and one new private helper), `tests/test_servo_undriven.py` (new). Nothing else.

## Why (red on main)
A design with a `servo` component and no mechanism gives no T6 row at all: `tier6_mechanisms` returns `None` when there are no `mechanisms[]` and no articulated attachments (checked on main with the U13.4-1 fixture minus its `mechanisms` and `articulation` blocks: tier 6 absent). The A400M had four servos driving nothing.

## Rule
For a design whose morphology is conventional (`from .applicability import NEEDS`; `NEEDS["conventional"](design.data["morphology"])` is true: not multirotor and `vtol_method` none), each component with `type == "servo"` that is NOT the parent of the driver attachment of any mechanism gets one row `CheckResult(f"T6.servo_undriven.{cid}", WARN, msg, CRIT, {"servo": cid})`, `msg = f"servo {cid} is not the driver of any mechanism (no mechanisms[] entry has a driver attachment whose parent is {cid}); see docs/mechanisms.md"`. "Driver attachment" = the attachment named by `mechanisms[i].driver`; its `parent` is compared with the servo id. Designs that are not conventional (every existing design with servos: concept-a, concept-a-param are tailsitters, c0-* are lift-plus-cruise) get no new row.

## Exact steps (`core/hangar/gates/mechanisms.py`)
1. Add `def _undriven_servos(design) -> list[str]` implementing the rule (ids in component order; `[]` when not conventional).
2. In `tier6_mechanisms`: compute `idle = _undriven_servos(design)` right after `arts`; change the early return to `if not mechs and not arts and not idle: return None`; build the existing `res` as today, then append (before `return TierReport(...)`) the servo rows for `idle`. Nothing else changes (the order of existing rows stays; a design with no idle servo yields exactly the old rows).
