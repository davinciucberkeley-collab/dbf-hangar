## What is wrong today
Bolted joints (`method: bolted`) show no fastener: concept-a has dozens of them and c172-rc ten, and nothing in the model shows where a screw goes (gap G11). Attachments already carry an optional `connection` id (for example `CT-M3-SHCS-NYLOC`) whose entry in `standards/connection-types.yaml` has a `spec` text naming the screw size ("M3 SHCS through clearance hole, nyloc nut").

## Decision (default taken)
- Switch: `display.screw_heads: true` (absent = nothing drawn; existing designs byte-identical; concept-a has 142 `connection` ids and must NOT change).
- Which attachments: `method == "bolted"` AND a `connection` that exists in `hangar.gates.interfaces.load_connection_types()` AND whose `spec` contains a size matched by `re.search(r"\bM(\d+(?:\.\d+)?)", spec)` (`d` = the number, mm). Others are skipped silently (the connection check reports unknown ids).
- Where: the child component's parts (`component_parts`), concatenated; `q, _, tri = trimesh.proximity.closest_point(mesh, [location])`; `n = mesh.face_normals[tri[0]]` (unit, outward). The head sits on the child's surface nearest the attachment `location`: `hinge.cylinder_between(q, q + d * n, 0.9 * d, 24)` (head diameter 1.8 d, height d: a drawing rule, not ISO 4762, constants at the top of `visuals.py`). A `location` inside the child snaps to the nearest face (state this limitation in the docs bullet).
- ONE node `screws` (all heads concatenated), kind `"screw"`, rgba `(120, 120, 128, 255)`, `point_mm` = the first head's `q`; metadata via the S8 hook; never a component; it does not move in Physics (state it in the docs).
- Out of scope: thread, hex socket, washers, nuts, fastener mass, heads on attachments without `connection`, moving heads with articulated parts.
