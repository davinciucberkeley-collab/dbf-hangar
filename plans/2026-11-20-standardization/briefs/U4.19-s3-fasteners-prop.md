| type | key | quantity, unit | direction | what to search |

| CT-A-M25-SHCS-MOTOR (4 x M2.5 SHCS through 2.9 mm holes in a printed cup into the T-Motor base) | `rated_load_n` | N ultimate | tension per screw; pull-through of the printed hole is separate | engineeringtoolbox ISO 898-1 table, M2.5 row (stress area, proof load, minimum tensile load); if absent, another page that carries an ISO 898-1 M2.5 row. Pull-through of a printed PETG/PLA hole: only a test page that states a force for a similar screw and sample count. |
| CT-HW-SEATED (loose screw, nut or washer seated in a hole; carries no load of its own) | none | n/a | n/a | No search. Write the row `pending: no opened source`, reason "seating relation named in collide.py MOVE_VIA; no load-bearing rating exists". |

A stall torque is the servo's output, not a spline rating: record it in the Notes cell under its own name and say so. |
