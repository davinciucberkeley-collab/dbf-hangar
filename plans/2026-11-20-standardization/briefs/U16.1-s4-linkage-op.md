## What is wrong today
Every linkage is hand-coded: the A400M has 4 x (servo, arm, rod, horn, pin) = 20 components and 24 attachments with literal coordinates (e.g. `horn-ele [1665, 66, 379.05]`); c172-rc the same. A wing change (U15.1) moves the surface, not the linkage. No op generates one. Builder rules from recon E (forum opinion, so defaults and never thresholds): arm and rod at 90 degrees at neutral, rod perpendicular to the hinge line, horn hole over the hinge line.

## Decision (default taken)
Op `design.add-linkage` (CLI `plane design add-linkage`), module `core/hangar/ops/linkage.py`:
`plane design add-linkage DESIGN --servo SERVO_ID --hinge ATTACHMENT_ID --station S [--prefix P] [--arm-length MM] [--horn-height MM] [--arm-side +|-] [--horn-dir X,Y,Z] [--input-range DEG] [--servo-range DEG] --why TEXT`
Defaults: `--prefix` = the hinge attachment id; `--arm-length` 6; `--horn-height` 13; `--arm-side` `+`; `--horn-dir` `0,0,-1`; `--input-range` 20; `--servo-range` 30. (All are layout defaults for a 1:10 RC model, not thresholds; the PR text says so.)
Inputs, read from the RESOLVED design (`load_design` + `resolve_design_data`): servo component `SERVO_ID` with a library part that has `frames.output` (S3); its output frame in the body frame `O` (point) and `a_o` (unit axis) = `R @ output.point + position`, `R @ output.axis` with `R = geometry.rotation_matrix(rotation_deg)`; the hinge attachment (must have `articulation`): `pivot` `P0`, unit `axis` `ax`, and its child (the control surface) `S_id`; `--station S` = distance in mm along `ax` from `P0` (signed): `P = P0 + S * ax`.
Construction (all numbers rounded to 4 decimals before writing):
1. Horn pin: `d = (O - P)` minus its component along `ax`; `n = unit(ax x d)`; flip `n` so that `n . horn_dir >= 0`; input error `give --horn-dir` when `abs(n . unit(horn_dir)) < 0.3`; `pin0 = P + horn_height * n`.
2. Arm tip: `v = pin0 - O`; `w = v - (v . a_o) a_o`; `W = |w|`; error when `W <= arm_length` (`arm length must be shorter than the distance from the servo shaft to the horn pin`); `phi = acos(arm_length / W)`; `e = cos(phi) * w/W + side * sin(phi) * (a_o x w/W)` (`side` = +1 or -1); `tip0 = O + arm_length * e`. (Then `(pin0 - tip0) . (tip0 - O) = 0`: arm and rod are at 90 degrees at neutral in the plane normal to the shaft.)
3. Components (all `type: other`, ids `<P>-arm`, `<P>-rod`, `<P>-horn`, `<P>-pin`): arm = `strut` from `O` to `tip0`, `diameter 1.5`, `chord 4`; rod = `strut` from `tip0` to `pin0 - 2.0 * unit(pin0 - tip0)` (2 mm short of the pin, as the recipe leaves the rod unattached at the horn end), `diameter 1.5`; horn = `strut` from `P` to `pin0`, `diameter 1.5`, `chord 4`; pin = `strut` from `pin0 - 0.6 t` to `pin0 + 0.6 t` with `t = unit(unit(pin0 - tip0) x n)`, `diameter 1.5`. Each: `position: [0, 0, 0]`, `mass_g: 1` (pin 0.2), `manufacturing: {method: laser-cut, machine: jacobs-laser}` for arm and horn, `{method: purchased}` for rod and pin, `material` omitted.
4. Attachments: `<P>-servo-arm` {parent SERVO_ID, child `<P>-arm`, method bolted, location `O`, embed_ok true, rated_load_n 10, articulation {type revolute, pivot `O`, axis `a_o`, range_deg [-servo_range, servo_range]}}; `<P>-arm-rod` {parent `<P>-arm`, child `<P>-rod`, method hinge, location `tip0`, embed_ok true, rated_load_n 10, kinematic_link true}; `<P>-horn-mount` {parent `S_id`, child `<P>-horn`, method glued, location `P`, embed_ok true, rated_load_n 10}; `<P>-horn-pin` {parent `<P>-horn`, child `<P>-pin`, method bolted, location `pin0`, embed_ok true, rated_load_n 10}.
5. Mechanism `<P>-drive`: `{id: <P>-drive, driver: <P>-servo-arm, kinematics: four_bar, driven: [HINGE], input_range_deg: [-input_range, input_range], expect: [{attachment: HINGE, ratio: <r>}], link: {driver_end: <P>-arm-rod, follower_end: <P>-horn-pin}}` with `<r>` = the small-angle ratio the gate itself computes, rounded to 2 decimals. Refactor `core/hangar/gates/mechanisms.py::_ratio_row` so its lever computation is a public function `link_ratios(design, mech) -> dict[str, float]` (attachment id -> geometric ratio, unrounded) that `_ratio_row` calls; behaviour and messages of `_ratio_row` stay byte-identical (proved by the hash check). The op builds the design with the four components and attachments in memory first, calls `link_ratios`, then writes.
6. Idempotent: for each of the 4 components, 4 attachments and 1 mechanism: if the id exists and the element equals the new one (compare after `yaml.safe_load`, numbers rounded to 4 decimals) skip it; if it exists and differs, replace it in place (same list position); else append. Use the existing byte-preserving helpers in `hangar.pdledit` (`patch_text` with the key `components[<id>]` etc., `append_element`); if `patch_text` cannot replace a whole mapping, replace its fields one by one with `patch_text` on `components[<id>].geometry.params.start`, `.end` and the attachment `location`/`articulation` keys, and say so in the PR. Everything in ONE write with one CHANGELOG entry per element (`why` from `--why`; reuse `_write_with_log`). A second identical call must leave `plane.yaml` and `CHANGELOG.yaml` byte-identical (it writes nothing and returns `changed: false`).
7. After writing, the op validates tiers 0 and 6 of the written design and returns in `data`: `components`, `attachments`, `mechanism`, `ratio`, `tip0`, `pin0`, `rod_length`, `min_transmission_deg` (from `simulate_mechanism` steps), and `rows` (the T0 FAIL rows and every `T6.*.<P>-drive` row as `[check_id, status, message]`). The op itself never fails because of a T6 FAIL (it reports it).
- Out of scope (state in the PR): choosing the servo position or the hinge station automatically; arm slot cavities; clevises and Z-bends (S9); non-`strut` parts; mechanisms with two driven surfaces (flaps); tail-surface hinge derivation (the op reads whatever the resolved hinge says).
