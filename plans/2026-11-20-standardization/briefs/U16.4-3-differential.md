## What is wrong today
`add-linkage` squares arm and rod at neutral (`_tip` puts the tip so that `(pin0 - tip) . (tip - Op) = 0`), so travel is nearly symmetric. Real aircraft use differential (C172 aileron up 20 / down 15). The standard RC way: at neutral turn the servo arm forward (toward the surface) by an offset angle, so one throw grows and the other shrinks. No PDL field is needed: the offset shows only in the written `tip0` and rod coordinates.

## Design (fixed; do not revisit)
- New option `--arm-offset-deg A` (registry `arm_offset_deg`, float, default `0.0`, degrees, signed). `A = 0` must give byte-identical output to today (same `plane.yaml` and `CHANGELOG.yaml`; `data` only gains the key below).
- Validation, before anything is written, `OpInputError` (file unchanged): `abs(A) > 60` gives `--arm-offset-deg must be between -60 and 60`; `phi_new` (below) not strictly between 0 and pi gives `--arm-offset-deg {A:g} swings the arm past the horn direction; lower it`.
- Construction. Everything in `_geometry` is unchanged (arm plane, `Op`, `K`, `n`, `q`, `H`, `pin0`, the pin side `sg` from the squared stage-A tip): the offset moves only the arm tip. Add a keyword `offset_deg: float = 0.0` to `_tip(pin, Op, a_o, arm_length, side)` (core/hangar/ops/linkage.py:88). Inside, after `phi = float(np.arccos(arm_length / W))` add `if offset_deg: phi = phi - float(np.radians(offset_deg))` and the range check above; the return line stays `Op + arm_length * (cos(phi) * wu + side * sin(phi) * cross(a_o, wu))`. So the tip is the squared tip rotated about the axis through `Op` along `a_o` by `A` degrees toward the horn direction `wu` (the projection of `pin0 - Op` on the arm plane); `A > 0` turns it toward the surface, `A < 0` away. Only the final call `tip0 = _tip(pin0, Op, a_o, arm_length, side, offset_deg)` (line 191, after `sg` is fixed) gets the offset; the stage-A call that chooses `sg` does not. Thread `arm_offset_deg` through `add_linkage_op`, `_add_linkage` and `_geometry` as a keyword with default `0.0`.
- Everything downstream follows from the new `tip0`: arm strut `Op -> tip0`; rod strut `tip0 -> pin0 - 2.0 * unit(pin0 - tip0)` (ROD_SHORT: the rod still ends 2 mm short of the pin); attachments at `tip0` and `pin0`; mechanism unchanged in shape; `rod_length = |pin0 - tip0|` (shorter for A > 0).
- `expect.ratio` and the gate: the op writes the value the gate itself computes from the written points (existing code, `link_ratios(...)[hinge]` rounded to 2 decimals). For `A != 0` that is the small-angle ratio AT THE OFFSET NEUTRAL (the slope at input 0), not `arm / lever`, and not the follower angle over the input at either end. `T6.ratio` still applies and passes, because it compares the declared ratio with the same `link_ratios` computation. For a four_bar mechanism the gate does not compare the follower angle with `ratio x input` (`_mechanism_row` skips that loop when four_bar), so a differential does not trip it; what can trip it is the transmission angle (`four_bar_min_transmission_deg` 10 deg) and T1 clearance, reported as rows as today. No threshold changes.
- `data` gets `arm_offset_deg` (the float used, rounded to 4, `0.0` when not given) and `follower_deg` (`{"plus": f(+in), "minus": f(-in)}`, signed, rounded to 3, `None` on a jam) unless U16.4-4 already added it (it has: reuse it, do not add it twice). First message line: unchanged, plus `, arm offset {A:g} deg` appended before `; unchanged`, only when `A != 0`.
- Composition with `--deflection` (U16.4-4): the arm is solved first as for the squared arm, then the offset is applied; the reported `deflection_deg` is the achieved mean of the two throws (it comes out below the target for A != 0; no re-solve). Say so in the docs lines.
- Which way is "up": the sign of the follower angle is the right-hand rule on the hinge axis. For the fixture hinge `(0, -1, 0)` and for `wing-aileron-r` (axis `(0.1, -1, -0.03)`) a positive follower angle lifts the trailing edge (up); for `wing-aileron-l` (axis `(0.1, 1, -0.03)`) positive is trailing edge DOWN. Which throw grows for a given sign of A depends on arm side and servo orientation (opposite on the fixture and on the c172 aileron-r, see the tables): the designer reads the pair from `follower_deg` and picks the sign of A. Do not add logic to choose it.
- Update the op summary string: add "`--arm-offset-deg A` turns the arm toward the surface by A degrees at neutral for differential throw (default 0 = squared)."
