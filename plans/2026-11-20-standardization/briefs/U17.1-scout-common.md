## Search order for every value (stop at the first usable hit)
1. Manufacturer datasheet or catalogue (WebSearch the part family, then WebFetch the PDF or product page).
2. A published standard, regulation or handbook page you can open (FAA AC 43.13-1B, 14 CFR, MMPDS/MIL-HDBK, public ASTM/ISO table pages, NASA/NACA technical reports, university lecture notes or textbooks with a stated page).
3. A named RC or DBF-style reference that states its test or design condition (sample size, method, aircraft size). No forum posts, no AI summaries, no "typically about".
Budget: at most 8 page opens per value. Then give up on that value.

## What counts as a usable number
- Quantity, unit, exact condition (minimum or typical, direction, temperature, aircraft class), and the place (page and table or section, or URL plus heading). One short quoted phrase (a few words) at most; paraphrase the rest.
- Convert units only with exact factors (1 lbf = 4.44822 N, 1 in = 25.4 mm, 1 lb/ft^2 = 4.88243 kg/m^2, 1 oz/ft^2 = 0.305152 kg/m^2) and show both numbers.
- If the source states a range or a bound, copy the bound and say which kind it is (`minimum`, `maximum`, `typical range`, `definition`).
- `agrees` column: `yes` only if the current value equals the source's bound or lies inside a range the source states as acceptable; `no` if it lies outside; `n/a` if the source states no bound for this quantity. You do not argue the case.
