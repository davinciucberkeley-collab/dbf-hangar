**outline: re-brief before build** (class hard · role builder-plus · plan: §7 U16.1 step 3, S8; design note section 2 S8 and section 6 risk 3, gap G8 · branch `std/u16.1-s8-wires` · SLUG = `wires`)

**PDL 1.3 fields introduced HERE (no new bump; the schema is already 1.3 since S1): `wiring.harnesses[].from_port`, `to_port`, `waypoints` (list of vec3), `diameter_mm`** (diameter otherwise from `gauge_awg`). Library `part.yaml` gains `ports` (names with a part-local point and direction); the T0.version row of `_tier0_v13` is extended for them.

## Intent
Tube along a Catmull-Rom curve from port through waypoints to port, emitted as GLB nodes in a `wiring` group (visual only: `visual_only: true` metadata, derived at export, never in `components`, so no mass, no T1 overlap, no MJCF body: the wire mass stays in the existing point-mass allowances). T7 reports each wire's length and checks the polyline against the envelopes when waypoints exist (else as today, `core/hangar/gates/integration.py:15`). Ports added to servo, ESC, receiver, battery and motor library parts (source or `frames_source`-style estimate text, as S3). A bend-radius figure is reported only (`pending`, never a FAIL).
## Acceptance (to be made exact)
Scratch c172 with 6 harnesses (receiver to each of 4 servos, ESC to motor, battery to ESC): GLB has 6 wire nodes; T7 PASS; designs without waypoints byte-identical (export hashes); Studio shows the wires.
