# Brief U13.8-2-bom-webfetch: BOM `verified.method` accepts `webfetch`

class: easy · role: builder · plan: §7 U13.8 (rehearsal 4 gap 7, c172-rc) · depends on: nothing · merge order: 2nd · SLUG = `bom-webfetch`

Environment: follow `plans/2026-11-20-standardization/briefs/U13.7-env.md` with every `u13.7` read as `u13.8` and SLUG=bom-webfetch (Haiku trailer).

## Why (red on main)
Library parts carry `verified: {method: webfetch}` (every `knowledge/parts/*/part.yaml` written by the scouts, for example `knowledge/parts/sunnysky-x2814-v3-900kv/part.yaml`), but the BOM schema `$defs/bom_verified.method` is `enum [browser, datasheet, in-hand]` and `plane bom verify` takes the same three words. An agent that fetched the vendor page cannot record that on a BOM line, so rehearsal 4 left 17 lines unverified (T8 WARN x17). Red: `plane bom verify <design> <item> --method webfetch --evidence "..." --by x` fails with `Invalid value for '--method'`, and a plane.yaml line `verified: {by: a, date: "2026-11-25", method: webfetch}` fails the schema (`'webfetch' is not one of ['browser', 'datasheet', 'in-hand']`).

## Decision (default taken)
`webfetch` is accepted and counts like the other methods in T8 (the freshness rule and the message `(webfetch)` come from `core/hangar/gates/sourcing.py` line 114, which prints the method and needs no change): the evidence field says what was fetched. A reviewer who wants "browser only" can change the rule later; no new T8 row is added.
