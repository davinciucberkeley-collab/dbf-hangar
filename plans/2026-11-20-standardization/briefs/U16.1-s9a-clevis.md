## What is wrong today
The pushrod is a bare 1.5 mm rod that ends in the air 2 mm short of the horn pin (c172-rc: `rod-elev` ends at x 736, the pin is at 738) and nothing shows how it is fastened (gap G11). A real linkage has a clevis (a forked sleeve with a cross pin) at each end.

## Decision (default taken)
- Switch: `display.clevises: true` (default absent = no clevis; every existing design stays byte-identical).
- For each `mechanisms[]` entry that has a `link`, a driver attachment (label = `mechanism.driver`) with an `articulation`, and a first driven attachment (`driven[0]`) with an `articulation`: `d0` = the `location` of the attachment named `link.driver_end`, `f0` = the `location` of `link.follower_end`, `u = unit(f0 - d0)`; `a_d` = unit axis of the driver articulation, `a_f` = unit axis of the `driven[0]` articulation. Mechanisms missing any of these are skipped silently.
- TWO nodes per mechanism (so Physics can move each with its own part): `clevis-<mechanism id>-driver` = barrel `hinge.cylinder_between(d0, d0 + 8 * u, 1.5, 24)` plus cross pin `cylinder_between(d0 - 2.5 * a_d, d0 + 2.5 * a_d, 0.6, 24)`; `clevis-<mechanism id>-follower` = barrel `cylinder_between(f0, f0 - 8 * u, 1.5, 24)` plus cross pin `cylinder_between(f0 - 2.5 * a_f, f0 + 2.5 * a_f, 0.6, 24)`. (Drawing dimensions, not from a standard: barrel diameter 3 mm and 8 mm long, pin diameter 1.2 mm and 5 mm long, for 1.5 mm rods at 1:10 scale; module constants.) Each node: the two meshes concatenated (`trimesh.util.concatenate`), kind `"clevis"`, rgba `(170, 172, 180, 255)`, `point_mm` = `d0` / `f0`; GLB metadata `{"type": "clevis", "mass_g": 0, "visual_only": true}` through the S8 hook. Never a component: no mass, no T1 row, no MJCF body (the clevis mass stays in the pushrod's allowance).
- Physics: in `mechanism_sweeps`, for each step of mechanism `m` add `transforms["clevis-<m>-driver"]` = the transform of the component that is the child of `link.driver_end` (the rod) and `transforms["clevis-<m>-follower"]` = the transform of the child of `link.follower_end` (the pin), each only when that component has a (non-identity) transform in the step. The viewer applies transforms by node name, so the clevises then ride with the rod and the horn.
- Known limitation (report it to the conductor, do not fix): U19.1 B2's tree lists every `visual_only` part under its group named Wiring, so clevises appear there until the viewer renames the group.
