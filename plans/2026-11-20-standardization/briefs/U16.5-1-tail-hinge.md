## What is wrong today
`hinge_from: generator` only accepts a child `wing_panel` elevon. An elevator or rudder (generator `tail_surfaces`, or `fin` part rear) gives the error "child must be a wing_panel part elevon...". The designer types pivot and axis by hand; the rehearsal got the elevator pivot wrong once (y 0 is in the 24 mm `elevator_fin_gap`, so T1.articulation said "27.0 mm from elevator") and computed the rudder axis by hand.

## Design (fixed)
1. `core/hangar/geom/tails.py`: split the first block of `_build` (the dict `stab` and the `hf`/`part` handling up to and including the `layout == "v"` update, lines 23-44) into `def _stab_params(p: dict) -> dict` that returns `stab`; `_build` calls it and continues unchanged. The meshes produced for every existing parameter set must stay byte-identical (export-hash check).
2. `tails.py`: new `def hinge_line(params: dict, base_dir) -> tuple[list[float], list[float]]` (component-local frame, mm, same contract as `wing.hinge_line`):
   - `p = fill_defaults(get("tail_surfaces"), params)` (import as `wing.py` does: `from hangar.geom.registry import fill_defaults, get`).
   - `part == "elevator"`: `stab = _stab_params(p)`; `stab["side"] = "right"`; `q0, q1 = hinge_line` of `wing_panel` (`from hangar.geom.wing import hinge_line as wing_hinge_line`) on `stab`; add `p["stab_x_le"]` to both x values; return. The hinge line therefore runs over the RIGHT half of the elevator between `elevon_y` = `[y0, y1]` (with `elevator_fin_gap` g this is `[fin_thickness/2 + g, stab_span/2]`), so its mid point lies on the elevator even when the gap leaves the centre empty.
   - `part == "rudder"`: layout `v` or `h` raises `GeometryError("hinge_from: rudder needs layout conventional or t")`. Otherwise call `fin_hinge_line` (step 3) with the fin keys exactly as `_build` builds them (`thickness, root_chord, tip_chord, height, x_le, z_root, sweep_le_deg`, `plane_y` 0, `part` "rear", `hinge_frac`).
   - any other part: `GeometryError("hinge_from: tail_surfaces part must be elevator or rudder")`.
   - `GeometryError` is `hangar.geom.kernel.GeometryError`.
3. `core/hangar/geom/parts.py`: new `def fin_hinge_line(params: dict, base_dir=None)`: `p = fill_defaults(get("fin"), params)`; `p["part"] != "rear"` raises `GeometryError("hinge_from: fin part must be rear")`; `dx = abs(h) * tan(sweep_le_deg)`; returns `[x_le + f*root_chord, plane_y, z_root]` and `[x_le + dx + f*tip_chord, plane_y, z_root + h]` (the same two points `_fin_rear_hinged` uses for `q0`, `q1`).
4. `core/hangar/geom/resolve.py` `_derive_hinges`: accept child generators `wing_panel` (unchanged path), `tail_surfaces` (`tails.hinge_line`) and `fin` (`parts.fin_hinge_line`); update the `generic` message to `child must be a wing_panel part elevon with side left or right, a tail_surfaces part elevator or rudder, or a fin part rear, and the attachment needs an articulation`. Axis sign rule: normalise `p1 - p0`; if `abs(axis[1]) > 1e-6` make `axis[1] >= 0` (as today: wing, elevator), else make `axis[2] >= 0` (rudder). Pivot, location and axis are rounded to 4 decimals as today. A wing aileron must give exactly the values it gives today (`tests/test_derived_hinge.py` passes unchanged).
5. Sign consequence to write into the schema text and the team guide: derived elevator axis points to +y, so a hand-written `axis: [0, -1, 0]` with `range_deg: [-29, 22]` becomes `range_deg: [-22, 29]` after opting in (positive = trailing edge up either way after the flip; check the written range with `plane sim`). Do not touch any design.
6. `schema/plane.schema.json` `hinge_from.description`: append " The child may also be a tail_surfaces part elevator (right half; the pivot lies on the elevator even with elevator_fin_gap) or rudder, or a fin part rear; the derived axis points to +y (elevator) or +z (rudder), so a typed range_deg written for a -y axis must be mirrored." Then `plane schema docs --out docs/pdl/reference.md`; `git diff docs/pdl/reference.md` shows only that row. `docs/team-guide.md` line 216: replace "servo, arm, rod and horn parts and tail surfaces do not follow yet" with "servo, arm, rod and horn parts do not follow yet; elevators, rudders and fin rear parts follow too (see the PDL reference for the axis sign)". Nothing else on that line.
