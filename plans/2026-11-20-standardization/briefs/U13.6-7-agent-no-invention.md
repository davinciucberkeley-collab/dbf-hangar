## Why (rehearsal 2: C172 gap 11, A400M gaps 4 and 10)
To clear `T2.sim_drop_loads` the C172 agent raised joint `rated_load_n` values "with no connection type behind them"; the motor `propulsion` numbers were estimates written as data; prices were left empty only because nothing existed. A check that is cleared by an invented number is a false pass and hides the real gap from the design owner.

## Exact steps (`.claude/agents/design-engineer.md`)
1. In `## Never`, after the line `- Relax a threshold, or add a part to make a check pass without the written reason.` insert these two lines:
```
- Invent a number to clear a check: no joint `rated_load_n`, `max_current_a`, `max_thrust_n`, price, mass or requirement that does not come from a datasheet, a test, a library part or the task. When no source exists, leave `rating_basis: placeholder` (or the field absent / `pending` as the schema allows), let the check SKIP or WARN, and list it in the report.
- Raise a rating, shrink a load or widen a requirement only with a cited source and a one-line reason in `--why`; "to make T2 pass" is not a reason.
```
2. In `## Output`, append to the paragraph a new line: `Also a list "Unsourced values": every number you wrote that has no source, with its key and why it is a placeholder or estimate, and every check left SKIP or WARN because of it.`
3. Nothing else in the file changes.
