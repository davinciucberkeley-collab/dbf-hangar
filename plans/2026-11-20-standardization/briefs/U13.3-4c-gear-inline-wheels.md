## Exact steps
1. Params: `"main_wheels": ParamSpec("int", default=1, minimum=1, doc="main wheels in line per side, count")`, `"wheel_pitch": ParamSpec("number", default=0.0, minimum=0, doc="x distance between neighbouring main wheel centres, mm")`.
2. `_gear_build`: for `main_wheels == 1` the code is unchanged (bit-identical result). Otherwise, per side `s`: the leg stays at `main_x` (top and axle as now); wheel `i` (0..n-1) has its axle at x = `main_x + (i - (n - 1) / 2) * wheel_pitch`, same y and axle z, `kernel.tube("y", axle_i, ww, wd)`; one bogie beam `kernel.tube("x", (main_x, s * track / 2, axle_z), (n - 1) * wheel_pitch, ld)` joins the wheel centres; all united with the rest as now.
3. `_gear_check`: add `"wheel_pitch: must be > wheel_d when main_wheels > 1"` when `main_wheels > 1` and `not wheel_pitch > wheel_d`.
4. (U13.4) New param `"wheel_shape": ParamSpec("choice", default="disc", choices=("disc", "tyre"), doc="disc = plain cylinder wheel; tyre = rounded tyre with recessed hub")`. `disc` is the existing `kernel.tube("y", axle, ww, wd)` for every wheel (main and nose; bit-identical result). `tyre`: replace each wheel by `kernel.revolve("y", axle, profile)` where `profile` is the closed list of `(a, r)` points (a = axial offset from the axle centre along y, r = radius) produced by a private helper `_tyre_profile(d, w)`: `R = d/2`, `h = w/2`, `rec = 0.2*w`, `rh = 0.5*R`, `rc = min(0.35*w, 0.5*(R - rh))`; points in order: `(-h+rec, 0)`, `(-h+rec, rh)`, `(-h, rh)`, `(-h, R-rc)`, then the quarter arc of radius `rc` about `(-h+rc, R-rc)` from angle 180 deg to 90 deg in 6 steps (points `(-h+rc + rc*cos t, R-rc + rc*sin t)`, t = 180, 165, ..., 90 deg, skipping t = 180 which is already listed), `(h-rc, R)`, the arc about `(h-rc, R-rc)` from 90 deg to 0 in 6 steps (t = 90 skipped, 75 ... 0), `(h, rh)`, `(h-rec, rh)`, `(h-rec, 0)`. For `wheel_shape = "tyre"` the main axle beam and legs are unchanged.
5. (U13.4) New param `"front_attach_z": ParamSpec("number", doc="z where the nose or tail leg top meets the airframe; omitted = attach_z")`. In `_gear_build` the front leg top is `(front_x, 0.0, p.get("front_attach_z", p["attach_z"]))` instead of `(front_x, 0.0, az)`; everything else unchanged. `_gear_check` adds `"front_attach_z: must exceed ground_z by more than the front wheel diameter"` when given and `not front_attach_z - ground_z > fw`.

## Tests: `tests/test_gear_inline_wheels.py`

2. `test_three_wheels_volume`: `main_wheels=3, wheel_pitch=60`: one watertight part; `vol(n=3) - vol(n=1) == pytest.approx(2 * (2 * Vw + 2 * (60 - 40) * Vb1), rel=1e-3)` (two sides; two extra wheels and two visible beam gaps of pitch minus wheel_d each).
