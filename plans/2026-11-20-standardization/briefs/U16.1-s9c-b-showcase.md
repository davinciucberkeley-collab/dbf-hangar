## What and why
With `wall: 3` the c172-rc fuselage is a 3 mm shell (outer surface unchanged, mass 720 g to 617 g). Nine joint points sat inside the old solid foam and now fail T1 (`T1.attachment_location` x6, `T1.attachment_contact` x3, plus three `T1.floating` WARNs). The fix is below: six joint points move onto the skin (no part moves), battery, ESC and receiver move straight down until they touch the inner skin and their joint points move to the contact point. No part is added. Measured on a scratch copy (Part A worktree core 557a7ad, c172-rc as on main at b3663f4): 0 new FAIL, 0 new WARN in tiers 0, 1, 3, 6.
