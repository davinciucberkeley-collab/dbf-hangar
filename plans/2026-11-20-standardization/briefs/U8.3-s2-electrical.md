## Per type: what the gate needs and what to look for
| type | key | quantity, unit | what to search |
|---|---|---|---|
| CT-AS150U (Amass anti-spark pair; the library holds `rated_current_a: 75` with source pending; its spec says "70 A continuous, 140 A momentary per maker") | `rated_current_a` | continuous A, one mated pair | Amass catalogue or product sheet, "AS150U" specifications table. | CT-BULLET-3 (3 gold bullet connectors, motor phases; size per design: 3.5, 4 or 5.5 mm) | `rated_current_a` | continuous A per bullet | manufacturer or distributor datasheet for 3.5 mm, 4 mm and 5.5 mm gold bullets. | CT-SOLDER-PHASE, CT-SOLDER-DC (soldered wire joints with heat-shrink) | `rated_current_a` | A | a published test or standard giving the current of a soldered wire joint (IPC-2221 tables, a lab or university test, a named test blog stating method and samples). | CT-SERVO-JR (JR/Futaba 3-pin servo connector) | `rated_current_a` | A per contact | Futaba/JR/Hitec connector spec, or the Molex 2.54 mm KK or TE datasheet current per contact. Record which pitch and contact family the page is for. |
