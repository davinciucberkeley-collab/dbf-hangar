## Why (checked on main, 2026-11-24)
`knowledge/parts/tattu-2300mah-4s-75c/mesh.stl` holds two bodies after `merge_vertices()` and `split(only_watertight=False)`: the pack (158 faces, volume 106,353 mm3, bounds x -62.25..-25.25, y -61.88..44.83, z -18.10..18.10) and a lead stub (52 faces, volume 1,505 mm3, bounds x 48.45..62.25, y 46.38..61.88, z -14.87..-6.77) about 95 mm from the pack. The mesh bounding box is centred on the origin (that is how the envelope `dimensions_mm: [124.5, 123.8, 36.2]` was made), so the PACK is centred at (-43.75, -8.5, 0): joints placed at the part origin miss the pack (A400M gap 5: FC joint 7.7 mm off, battery offset about (-43, -8, -4) mm) and the stub pokes out of the fuselage side (C172 gap 10). The other six library meshes are single bodies centred on their envelope.

## Exact steps
1. Make the new mesh with this throw-away snippet (run it from the repo root with the worktree python; do NOT commit the snippet):
```
import trimesh
from pathlib import Path
p = Path("knowledge/parts/tattu-2300mah-4s-75c/mesh.stl")
m = trimesh.load(p, force="mesh"); m.merge_vertices()
pack = max(m.split(only_watertight=False), key=lambda b: b.volume)
pack.apply_translation(-(pack.bounds[0] + pack.bounds[1]) / 2.0)
assert pack.is_watertight and pack.is_volume
pack.export(p)
print(pack.extents.round(3), round(pack.volume))
```
   Expected print: extents about `[37. 106.71 36.2]`, volume 106353.
2. `part.yaml`: set `dimensions_mm: [37.0, 106.7, 36.2]`; append to `cad_source` the text `; U4.41: lead stub removed, pack re-centred on its bounding box (origin = pack centre; long axis y)`. Nothing else changes (`mass_g` 270 stays; the BOM and `verified` stay).
