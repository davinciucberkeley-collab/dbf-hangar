## What the diff does
`core/hangar/gates/discipline.py` `control_authority`: per axis the control moment is `min(M(+), M(-))`, where `M(+/-)` is the LP maximum of `+/- sum_i c_i dT_i` (`c_i = tau_i . axis`) subject to `sum_i (u_i . up) dT_i = 0` and `-T <= dT_i <= Tmax_i - T` (`scipy.optimize.linprog`, method highs; an infeasible solve gives 0). `m_wind`, thresholds and the pitch/roll status rules are unchanged. Yaw: PASS when the ratio is at least `hover_ctrl_factor`; otherwise SKIP (message ends "not judged: yaw from prop reaction torque is not modelled (PDL has no spin/torque field)"), `data.would_be = FAIL`. The yaw row also carries `data["trim_yaw_nm"] = T * sum_i tau_i . up` (rounded to 3 dp) and the text "yaw moment at equal hover thrust ... (not judged); yaw wind moment uses the pitch/roll model (conservative)". A pending criterion still SKIPs through `_judge` for PASS rows.

Defaults taken (the advice leaves these open):
- A yaw ratio below the factor is SKIP even when the criterion is not pending (advice, status rules). In report-only mode a SKIP shows as WARN.
- `trim_yaw_nm` is rounded to 3 dp; the message prints it with 3 dp.
- The tests read the control moment from the message text (`control moment <x> N*m`) because the advice adds no `data` key for it.
- The concept-a regression test is marked `slow` (loads the real design; CI runs it, a local default run skips it).
- The pinwheel fixture sets the cant sign from the sign of each motor's z (so every cant torque about `up` has one sign). A plain "same cant on all four" does NOT give a pinwheel in this fixture, because the upper and lower motors sit on opposite sides of the CG; do not simplify it.
- No new scipy dependency (already in `pyproject.toml`).
