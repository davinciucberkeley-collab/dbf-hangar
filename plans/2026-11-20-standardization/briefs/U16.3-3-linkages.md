## Why
concept-a's elevon servos are imported STL meshes and its two linkages (servo arm, pushrod, horn, pin, six hand-made parts per side) are hand-modelled. With T165 the part `emax-es3054hv-servo` has `frames` and `ports`, so the servos become library parts and `plane design add-linkage` generates the arm, rod, horn and pin, their attachments and a four_bar mechanism.
