## Decision (default taken) and the numbers behind it
Measured by W34-T5 on 2026-11-25 with a scratch MJCF of the `four-bar` layout (fixture components of `mechanism-recipe`), MuJoCo 4.0.1 as installed (`pyproject` requires `mujoco>=3`): with gravity 0, timestep 0.001, integrator `implicitfast`, position actuator `kp=2 kv=0.02`, hinge `armature=1e-6 damping=1e-5`, ball joint `armature=1e-8 damping=1e-7`, `connect` `solref="0.005 1"`, the servo settles in under 0.5 s and the elevator hinge angle equals the S1 solve to 4 decimals for input -20, -10, 10, 20 deg: 8.1215, 4.0154, -3.8240, -7.3789. The arm hinge axis `[0,0,1]` and the elevator hinge axis `[0,-1,0]` are skew (not parallel), so this also proves the ball joint at the rod end avoids the over-constraint of design note section 6 risk 1. With gravity on (-9.81) the elevator sags 0.4 to 0.65 deg (nothing holds it but the rod), so the kinematic export uses **gravity 0** (a documented choice: this file is for moving the mechanism, the drop test is `mjcf`).
Format rules, in this order (all lengths in the MJCF are metres, `angle="degree"`; positions are the design-frame coordinates / 1000, no body is offset or rotated: every body is `pos="0 0 0"` with identity rotation, joints and geoms carry absolute coordinates):
1. Tree: breadth-first from `design.data["root"]` along attachments `parent -> child`; the first attachment that reaches a child is its tree edge; an attachment to an already reached child is ignored (loop closure is done by the `connect`, below). Components not reached are merged into the root body.
2. Tree edge kinds: with `articulation` (`type` `revolute`, or `continuous` from S2) -> a hinge joint; without `articulation` but `kinematic_link: true` -> a ball joint at the attachment `location`; anything else -> rigid: the child component joins the parent's body (no `<weld>`, no `<freejoint>` anywhere). A new body starts at every hinge or ball edge; it holds the child and everything rigidly attached below it. Body name = the id of its head component (the root component for the root body).
3. Body content: one `<geom type="mesh" mesh="<cid>_m<i>" contype="0" conaffinity="0"/>` per part of each member component (mesh = the convex hull vertices of the part in metres, as `_build` does but absolute, not relative to the CG), one `<inertial>`: `mass` = sum of member `mass_g` / 1000, `pos` = mass-weighted mean of `component_cg(c, parts) / 1000`, `fullinertia` from `_component_inertia(mass, all member parts, cg_mm)` (reuse; for a body with no parts use `1e-9` on the diagonal as `_component_inertia` does). The root body has no joint (fixed in the world).
4. Joints: hinge: `<joint name="<label>" type="hinge" pos="<pivot/1000>" axis="<axis>" range="<lo> <hi>" limited="true" armature="1e-06" damping="1e-05"/>` (continuous: no `range`, `limited="false"`); ball: `<joint name="<label>" type="ball" pos="<location/1000>" armature="1e-08" damping="1e-07"/>`. `<label>` = `geometry.att_label(a)` (the attachment id, else `parent->child`). Positive angle is right-handed about the axis, the same convention as `geometry.joint_rotation` (the scratch run confirms it).
5. Actuators: for each `mechanisms[]` entry whose `driver` names an attachment with `articulation`: `<position name="<mechanism id>" joint="<driver label>" kp="2" kv="0.02" ctrllimited="true" ctrlrange="<lo_rad> <hi_rad>"/>` with `input_range_deg` converted to radians (`ctrl` is in RADIANS: `angle="degree"` converts joint ranges only). No `forcerange`.
6. Loop closure: for each mechanism with a `link`: `<connect name="<mechanism id>-link" body1="<body holding the child of link.driver_end>" body2="<body holding the child of link.follower_end>" anchor="<location of the follower_end attachment / 1000>" solref="0.005 1"/>` (anchor in body1's frame, which is the world frame at rest). Skipped when both bodies are the same body. No `<weld>`.
7. Wheels: a component that is the child of a `continuous` attachment is a wheel: its geoms get `contype="2" conaffinity="0" friction="1 0.005 0.0001"`; when at least one wheel exists add the first worldbody element `<geom name="ground" type="plane" size="5 5 0.1" pos="0 0 <z>" contype="1" conaffinity="2" friction="1 0.005 0.0001"/>` with `z` = the lowest vertex z of all wheel parts / 1000 (the plane touches the lowest wheel). The root stays fixed (wheels spin against the ground, nothing rolls away); a free-root rolling test is out of scope.
8. Header: `<mujoco model="<design id>-kinematic">`, `<compiler angle="degree"/>`, `<option timestep="0.001" gravity="0 0 0" integrator="implicitfast"/>`, then `<asset>` (meshes), `<worldbody>`, `<equality>` (only when there is a connect), `<actuator>` (only when there is a position actuator).
9. Out of scope (say so in the PR): `<weld>` loads (that is the `mjcf` drop test), gravity, a rolling gear test, mechanisms without a `link` (their driver gets an actuator; the driven joint is left free: no ratio coupling), propeller discs (no geoms collide; they are bodies like the rest).
