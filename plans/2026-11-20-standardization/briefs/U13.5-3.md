## Finding (dry run, reproduced by the tactician on this fixture)
- `geo.part_mesh(json.load(fixture))` raises `GeometryError: not watertight after union/cuts (watertight=False, volume=False, bodies=7, cuts=0)`. In the real import the 4 PRP-006 X-mounts (13 bodies, 4 cuts) fail the same way and the GLB fallback then also fails, so `plane import dbf` stops.
- Cause: the Manifold union of the 7 bodies is closed (`_closed(union)` True, volume 1657.559 mm3, 1253 vertices) but 2 pairs of vertices are at EXACTLY the same position (distance 0.0). `_separate_pinches` leaves them (after it: 2 pairs still within 1e-8). `_clean` then calls `mesh.merge_vertices(...)`, which welds each pair (1251 vertices) and creates 4 degenerate faces; the mesh is no longer closed. (Same root cause family as U13.5-4, a different place.)
