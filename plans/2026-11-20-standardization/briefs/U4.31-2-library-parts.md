## Exact steps and outputs
For each of the seven mesh parts:
1. `knowledge/parts/<id>/mesh.stl`: load `designs/concept-a/meshes/<component>.stl` with `trimesh.load(..., force="mesh")`, `apply_translation(-mesh.bounds.mean(axis=0))` (bounding-box centre to origin, NO rotation), export STL. Check `mesh.is_watertight and mesh.is_volume`; if not, stop.
2. `part.yaml` fields (template in `knowledge/parts/README.md`): `id`; `name` = the component's `description`; `category` from the table; `vendor` = BOM `supplier`; `part_number` = BOM `part_number` truncated at the first ` (` (keep the full text out); `url` = BOM `url` (if absent, stop); `mass_g` = the component's `mass_g`; `dimensions_mm` = `[round(e,1) for e in mesh.extents]` of the centred mesh; `cad_status: representative-envelope`; `cad_source: "designs/concept-a/meshes/<component>.stl (imported from DBF-Combined @ a93f14d; not vendor CAD)"`; `verified` = BOM `verified` block copied unchanged; `geometry: {generator: mesh_file, params: {file: mesh.stl}}`.
For the two envelope parts (motor, prop), no mesh file; compute with trimesh from the component's mesh the oriented-bounding-box extents `e = sorted(mesh.bounding_box_oriented.primitive.extents)`; the two closest values are the diameter `d` (their mean), the remaining one is the length `L`.
3. `tmotor-mn2212-v2-kv780`: `geometry: {generator: tube, params: {axis: x, length: L, outer_d: d}}`; `dimensions_mm: [L, d, d]`; `mass_g` 65 (the component's); same `cad_status`; `cad_source: "cylinder envelope from the oriented bounding box of designs/concept-a/meshes/prp-001-ll.stl"`.
4. `apc-9x4-5mr-prop`: `geometry: {generator: tube, params: {axis: x, length: t, outer_d: 228.6}}` where `t` = the smallest of the sorted extents of the mesh `prp-002-ll.stl`, `dimensions_mm: [t, 228.6, 228.6]`, `mass_g: 11.06` (value quoted in BOM `p02`); same marking and `cad_source` pointing at `prp-002-ll.stl`.
Round every written float to 1 decimal.
